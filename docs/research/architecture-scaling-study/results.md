## What changed in the next direction

Model admission is a concrete remaining obligation. Organizing signatures,
interpretations and satisfaction does not supply an interpretation of a source
calculus or prove that its constructors coexist in the chosen model. #366 gives
an explicit failed encoding and an admission inventory; it does not show that
all models fail. #289/#290 own the unchanged-source interpretation and connected
model comparison, while #36/#100 own joint higher/smooth constructor admission.
These can proceed without duplicating the external agent's Myers investigation.

Selected observations remain necessary. The ordinary geometry, higher-identity,
resource and partial-solution examples each preserve more than success or
existence, but the extra data differ. #364's possibility observation intentionally
forgets multiplicity; #365's marginal probability observation misses the stated
correlation. These are reasons to choose and prove the appropriate observation
contract, not to require one largest interface everywhere.

Some pressure tests favored existing interfaces. #361 met the trigger for a
nonconstant selected-certificate client, but the existing strict-map theorem
handled it. #358's native author also reused general laws and isolated the repair.
The named shared interface remains useful without being mandatory. The measured
native route had lower reported total stage time; shared used fewer checks and
had lower reported marginal times at the largest scale and repair. Configuration,
clock, host-load and one-author-per-arm limitations prevent a stronger claim.

The broader alternatives remain active where their distinct contracts are
needed. Nominal binding and higher-order probability were examined on their own
mathematical assumptions. Resource modes were checked against an independent
ownership contract; SDT was compared with external ordered domains. These
comparisons do not merely count successful adapters to the incumbent engine.
Their unresolved model constructions and different morphism classes matter.
