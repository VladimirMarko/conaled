## Requirements before module choices

The reusable mathematical object should be an argument with explicit admissible rules, not an argument that accidentally inherits every rule of the host. The following obligations constrain any candidate, including a replacement.

1. Interpret one dependent argument in distinct settings with stated equality and substitution laws. Separate a supplied capability from consequences derived uniformly from it. An extensional instance alone cannot certify an intensional claim.
2. Carry a selected identity witness, a family depending on it, and the induced dependent action through two changes of context. Compare the resulting types as well as the terms. Agreement of endpoints or mere existence of some witness does not satisfy this requirement.
3. Admit extensions over a common reduct and check their interaction on a joint interpretation. Inclusion of old derivations is different from conservativity, existence of expansions of every old model, and compatibility of two independently chosen expansions.
4. Hide representation relative to a declared observation. A compiler may retain a separate representation-inspection interface. A higher-order client relation or an equivariant section is an appropriate observation; a selected scalar packet is insufficient coverage.
5. Account separately for construction of semantic models, reusable interpretation proofs, repeated author work, and maintenance when rules change. A theorem quantified over arbitrary derivations removes one kind of repeated proof; it does not measure agent effort or the number of model-builder obligations.

These requirements permit a different source calculus, semantic category, equality discipline, or implementation host. An adapter into today's guarded syntax is not an acceptance condition for an alternative.

## The foundational alternatives answer different questions

A comprehension category is a candidate common language for dependency and comparison. A category with families or contextual category can be a strict presentation useful to syntax. Replacing one label by another does not alone change mathematical expressiveness: the permitted objects, morphisms, logical structure and preservation laws decide that. Ahrens, Lumsdaine and North organize these frameworks using sub-2-categories and comparison functors. Their v2 explicitly corrects a missing discreteness hypothesis in Proposition 46. This is a practical reason to pin theorem statements and map notions rather than cite an informal equivalence of frameworks. [Primary source: arXiv:2412.19946v2, 5 June 2026, §§2–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.19946v2).

Local universes offer a substantive alternative to manually chosen strict substitution laws. Lumsdaine–Warren Theorem 3.4.1 assumes full comprehension, condition LF, and the appropriate weakly stable constructors. LF requires finite products and specified dependent exponentials along display maps; it is an ambient condition, not merely an object-language function constructor. The construction yields a split replacement with strictly stable constructors. It does not require an object-language universe. Section 4.1's broader extension discussion is heuristic and excludes type-equality rules. Its W-type treatment also makes dependence on previously admitted constructors explicit. [Primary source: arXiv:1411.1736v2, §§3.1, 3.4, 4.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1411.1736v2).

My assessment is that this offers a plausible reduction in recurring model-builder obligations when chosen eliminators are difficult to make substitution-stable. It does not by itself preserve a client's previously selected eliminator or prove functorial comparison across independently strictified settings. That additional contract must be tested. Conversely, a missing Lean implementation is an encoding cost, not evidence that the formulation is mathematically unsuitable.

Direct strict construction remains a sensible option where the desired operations already commute with substitution and the selected witnesses are transparent. Its risk is repetition: each new model and constructor may need another bespoke coherence proof. Coherent splitting is a separate candidate when a provider already has functorial comparison data for the whole constructor. A chosen classifying universe is attractive when classifiers and closure are independently needed. None is a universal default. This assessment has inspected the local-universes theorem in detail; it has not independently audited complete transfer proofs for the latter two routes.

The smallest useful correction is to state model-construction requirements independently of the incumbent source engine, including weak stability and the chosen comparison arrows, and compare two constructions of the same consumer. Keep the current strict interface as one possible output. If native use of the alternative repeatedly avoids obligations imposed by that output, a replacement core should remain eligible. Relational squares and representation observations can survive such a replacement under their own contracts; this need not force all compiler and internal-language work through one foundation.

## Discriminating problems and expected outcomes

**A dependent monodromy problem across presentations.** Specify a family with nontrivial monodromy, a selected path, a dependent section/certificate, and its action, then change context twice through nonidentical but equivalent presentations. Include a higher witness distinguishable from the identity. Run the same argument in an extensional setting and a higher setting, with the latter preserving the distinguishing observation. Compare direct strict construction against a weak semantic construction plus coherence machinery. The expected mathematical outcome is equality or coherent comparison of both transported types and selected terms for sequential versus composite change, with the nontrivial observation retained. These are proposed tests, not checked results here. A collapse to endpoint equality fails. A weak model whose preservation is available only up to specified higher comparison could favor replacing a strict-only admission boundary. Repeating today's single frozen example with prepared helpers would not discriminate this.

**Independent extensions that can fail jointly.** Start from group theory with a named element `a`. One extension requires `a*a = 1`; another requires `a*a*a = 1`. Each has a nontrivial model with `a ≠ 1`. A joint model with that retained inequality cannot exist: the two equations imply `a = 1`. This elementary proposed control exposes any mechanism that mistakes separate admission for compatible expansion. A companion positive case should construct a joint model and retain a selected dependent section over it. The test judges the proposed architecture's diagnosis and composition contract, not whether its syntax matches the incumbent. Separately, cross-constructor laws involving dependency should be tested, because disjoint signatures can still interact through shared types and substitution.

**Relational hiding with an equivariant higher-order client.** Let an interface expose a family of sections and their action under a groupoid of context symmetries. Replace the hidden presentation by an equivalent one carrying different chosen lifts. Require every admitted client consuming such a section to preserve a stated relation, including compatibility with reindexing and composition of symmetries. Expected success retains the natural comparison and section action; equality of objectwise endpoints is insufficient. Include a deliberately non-equivariant client, which must be rejected or placed under a separate representation-observing contract. This probes higher-order observation without reducing it to an arithmetic packet. The benchmark must choose the relation and counterexample before either architecture implements it.

For each problem, increase dependent-tail depth, constructor count and independently supplied settings separately. Record reusable infrastructure once, then each additional interpretation, interaction law and client repair. Change one admitted computation rule and count all required repairs, including adapters and checking time. These are proposed measurements. No asymptotic author-effort claim follows from a universal soundness theorem or a small completion trial.

## Orbifolds distinguish internal reasoning from realization

Myers defines orbifolds using microlinearity and properly finite identity types. Theorem 6.3.7 concerns crisp ordinary proper étale groupoids in the paper's ambient synthetic theory. The identity data and crisp restrictions are part of that statement. The result should not be weakened to an orbit-set statement merely to obtain an easy interpretation. [Primary source: arXiv:2205.15887v1, 31 May 2022, Definition 1.1.2 and §6.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.15887v1).

For this study I separate four obligations. Internal derivation establishes a theorem from the synthetic rules. Model validity interprets those rules, modalities and infinitesimal principles in an actual semantic setting. Classical comparison relates internal quotients, properness and smooth structure to declared external presentations while retaining isotropy. Executable realization adds finite representations, algorithms and any approximation contract. Success at one obligation is evidence for that obligation; it is not a substitute for the others. Supplying synthetic principles as hypotheses is legitimate conditional mathematics.

A useful geometry discriminator is an action quotient with nontrivial stabilizer together with a dependent equivariant family, compared across a change of presentation and its composite. Retain the stabilizer action and selected comparison, then ask which geometric assumptions are needed to identify this construction with the synthetic object. Orbit sets intentionally fail this test. A satisfactory external groupoid construction can still fall short of internal microlinear reasoning; a satisfactory internal derivation can still leave the external comparison open. These are test requirements and possible outcomes, not claims about the incoming artifact.

## Decision limits

The consequential uncertainty is whether selected higher comparison data and the required modal restrictions can be obtained and reused more economically through general semantic coherence than through direct strict constructions. The inspected code proves neither answer. Lean remains a valid trusted implementation host for explicit semantic objects and interpretations; its native equality should not silently stand in for the setting's identity structure.
