## Exact Σ contract

`SumFilling.lean:11–72` and `CartesianDependentFilling.lean:137–198` implement the same dependent sequencing: fill the first projected box with F, obtain its boundary witness from hF, form the second box over that exact resulting section, fill it with G, and pair the sections. The witness enters the type of the second box. A comparison of first projections alone cannot transport this algorithm.

The construction requires F lawful because its boundary law constructs the second box. G need not be lawful to define the operation; G's lawfulness proves the output fills and is uniform. Both uniformity proofs identify a subtype consisting of the first section and its filling witness, then rewrite `OpenBox.sigmaSnd_reindex` and invoke G's uniformity. This is concrete evidence of a shared argument, with no De Morgan lattice operation in the Σ algorithm itself.

The box parameters differ materially. In `Filling.lean:9–20`, the source has a Cofibration and fixed false cap. In `CartesianFilling.lean:135–147,190–205`, the target has an arbitrary Subcontext and an interval section r as cap, reindexed to r[σ]. These parameters determine the base family and boundary equation. A common internal proof can parameterize admissible faces, cap sections and their substitution. Its source instance may choose a singleton cap parameter whose graph is the false endpoint; its target instance must retain every r. Specializing the target to false caps and finite faces would prove a useful restricted theorem but would not recover its native API.

A shared Σ interface needs comprehension and substitution, selected first and second filling operations, box projection and dependent second-box construction, pairing, and the box/pair reindexing equations. It should derive the resulting filling laws and exact selected output. Supplying `sigmaFilling_lawful` or preservation of the final Σ filler as fields would merely restate the intended result. Some box-level equations may reasonably be model obligations, but their independent derivation from cap/tube definitions must remain visible.

## Classification and recovery

The source `SmallFamilyGlue.lean:29–68` proves equality `decodedLocalFamily A.code = A`, including its chosen local filler. The Cartesian counterpart is `CartesianSmallFamilyClassification.lean:383–488`: it first proves operation preservation through `decodeCodeIso`, then family equality and filler HEq, then entire-record recovery and the two-sided equivalence. Both have local-to-global recovery (`LocalFillingBridge.lean:156,205`; `CartesianLocalFillingBridge.lean:163,225`). Therefore equality at all represented tests is enough to recover a lawful global filling operation. This is the strongest evidence for retaining the current APIs and extracting shared proofs behind them: the native contracts already preserve more than decoded fibers.

For a bounded specialization, let S be the local family obtained from the native lawful `sigmaFilling F hF G`, classify S, decode its code, and extend its decoded local operation globally. Whole-record recovery followed by the native local/global roundtrip derives recovery of that exact Σ operation. The source already packages S as `LocalFamily.sigma` (`SmallFamilyConstructors.lean:16–19`). The target can package its existing Σ filler using `LocalFamily.ofFilling`. Both conclusions are obtainable without assuming a cross-model interpretation. They should be stated as equality after the explicit family equality/transport, or HEq with the family equality separately exposed; ignoring the family endpoint would hide part of the contract.

This bounded result must not be presented as equality between the returned large-context constructor filler and `NativeDecode.filling` of its code. `LargeFamilyClassification.lean:74–195` supplies small representatives and chart assembly; its `nativeIso` is a family isomorphism. The constructor audit explicitly distinguishes the selected returned operation from the universal decoder choice. That stronger equality remains #607's scope unless independently proved.

A shared classifier must construct chart restriction, code assembly, generic evaluation, and operation recovery from naturality/locality. A record containing an encode/decode equivalence and roundtrip laws is an acceptable consumer interface, but instantiating it twice does not demonstrate shared derivation of classification. Report that distinction if the bounded implementation uses the existing classifiers without extracting their proof.

## Restriction and its obstruction

`CartesianRestriction.lean:17–53,95–99,223ff` gives restriction of contexts, families and sections, section injectivity, comprehension comparison and dependent-sum comparison. Its Π comparison is explicitly forward only (`:193–211`). These are useful structural comparisons, including dependent values; they are not a map between the two native Filling records.

`Examples/CartesianWitnessPreservation.lean:13–34` proves more precisely that there is no natural transformation from the restricted De Morgan represented interval to the Cartesian represented interval that fixes both zero-dimensional endpoints. Its proof applies naturality to source reversal and the target's three possible one-dimensional coordinate forms. It assumes no inverse or retraction equation. Thus it excludes an endpoint-preserving interval identification in this direction, and any proposed bridge requiring that map. It does not exclude maps in the other direction, bridges retaining the larger restricted interval, selected restricted classes of boxes, or relational comparisons. It also does not prove that common represented-classification reasoning is impossible.

A bridge claiming native selected Σ preservation must independently explain the cube functor, context/comprehension comparisons, interval comparison direction, admissible cap images, face images, restriction and reconstruction of both base and tube, and compatibility with dependent pairing. Then preservation of F and G on those translated component boxes can imply preservation of the composite Σ algorithm. Lawfulness alone cannot select an identical interior filler. Composition should quantify routes M→N and N→P, translate each box and its dependent endpoint family, and prove that their composed section comparison equals the direct composite route. There is no such general native-filler route in the inspected restriction file.
