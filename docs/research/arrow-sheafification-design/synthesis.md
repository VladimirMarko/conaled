# Selected arrow-sheafification case

All three independent assessments recommend the bounded one-object group route.
The [contract](contract.md), [use](use.md) and [alternatives](alternatives.md)
reports preserve their initial evidence and limits. They used fresh contexts and
the approved astra/high configuration, with two reports staged before the third
because a stale agent occupied one concurrency slot. Reports were not shared
between assessors. This supplies independent perspectives, not model diversity.

Retain the existing locally constant sheafification, lift its pointwise operations
to a group sheaf, and use the represented action on a terminal sheaf followed by
#727's general regular-bundle unit. Add the normalized presheaf of globally
constant functions to distinguish incompatible raw labels from a compatible
family that really fails to glue. The latter representation has checked scratch
support and needs no choice of a label on the empty open.

The reusable result here concerns small topological spaces and small groups.
General internal presheaf-groupoid sheafification and homwise pseudofunctor
prestackification remain different extensions. Sheafifying an object presheaf
can add objects before stackification, whereas homwise correction keeps objects.
Consequently their intermediate target classes differ. Neither is rejected
because the bounded client uses a simpler representation.

Two findings constrain the checked claims. Raw constant labels restrict by the
identity even at the empty open; different labels on disjoint opens do not
match there. Also, #727's singleton locally-surjective topology on sheaf
parameters does not include arbitrary jointly covering families or the empty
cover. The section-representation bridge does not transfer its universal
mapping theorem to every stack on the original open-set site. Preserve the
existing theorem and record the additional site-comparison contract separately.

Implementation must retain actual units, full arrows, parameter substitution,
nonempty isotropy, newly glued arrows and locally identified empty-open arrows.
The completed report will distinguish checked results from the broader
transformation/modification contract and give remaining proofs concrete owners.
No changes to existing callers or global proof-resource settings are indicated.
