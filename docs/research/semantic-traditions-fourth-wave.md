## Different obligations for selected computation

The [classical-control construction](classical-control-implementation.md)
separates three results. Logical adequacy works for its bounded implication/control
fragment over predecessor-closed poles. The constructed delivery pole admits
some selected outputs which fail the stronger first-match specification.
The independent finite-search certificate and machine proofs establish that
stronger specification for the actual search. Finally, a relation between
function callbacks and explicit continuation frames proves step correspondence
and retention of the complete result.

These distinctions prevent adequacy from being read as an arithmetic extraction
theorem. Search termination is proved for the bounded scanner, not arbitrary
lambda/control programs. The callback family is explicitly delimited; admitting
all host functions is not a consequence of this representation comparison.
Direct recursion remains an independent implementation with the same full
selected observation. Neither route has a measured productivity advantage.

The [retained-event comparison](retained-measurement-rollback.md) moves the
operation inside the protected computation. It uses the existing measurement
begin/record contracts and preserves the exact selected controller and ordered
history under both Keep and Undo. Local laws derive preservation through nested
handlers. Undo restores only tentative local state; it does not undo a retained
operation. Unknown completion remains an observation and does not establish
that no action occurred.

The model still requires an adapter to connect its issuance and observations
to the physical world. Agreement of native and interpreted calculations does
not discharge that correspondence, response availability or crash durability.
Those obligations remain with the concrete adapter and #225/#196; the history
list itself is not independent evidence of execution.

## What varying bodies add

The [nominal extension](nominal-varying-bodies.md) keeps two dependent binders
but adds a generated arithmetic body. Its selected output is explicitly the
residue in the dependent finite result type. Distinct bodies at the same free
parameters yield distinct selections through two substitutions. Support includes
free atoms occurring only in the body, and a checked capture mutation exposes
why type-bound support alone is insufficient.

Substitution traverses body syntax and connects to raw named substitution through
local concretion laws. The earlier projection and third-tail clients are retained
as specializations. The contextual route avoids support and frame obligations;
the nominal route supplies named syntax and capture avoidance on the same contract.
This does not establish arbitrary body-dependent typing, nested binders, or a
universal nominal frontend. The uniform residue contract is part of this bounded
experiment, not a solution to every dependent typing obligation.

## Reusing indexed transport

The [cursor comparison](indexed-relational-transfer.md) supplies a concrete case
where an existing abstraction already has the needed generality. The same-index
operation theorem is insufficient for changing capacities, but indexed theory
maps already provide the type action, local command certificates and composed
transport. The client adds no new generic interface.

Reserving capacity preserves the selected position and ordered history through
two index changes. An explicit associativity equality aligns the output types;
a relation between position values alone cannot justify that cast. The full
selected subtype result agrees with the independently frozen native calculation.
Reservation misses new target positions, so this preservation is not an equivalence.
The relation is graph-like once the target capacity is fixed; the experiment does
not establish arbitrary many-to-many dependent transport.

## Dependent extension at a restricted context

The [modal extension](modal-dependent-comprehension.md) reuses the existing
split-family model and connects its dependent sums to the restricted environments
from #375. Pairing and pullback uniqueness supply comprehension; key pullback
retains every dependent component. The concrete later type is `Fin 3` or `Fin 2`
depending on an accessible Boolean, so the variation cannot be dismissed as two
names for equivalent fibers.

Its source is deliberately shallow: Lean families and local functions over the
restricted carrier supply primitives, while structural terms and substitutions
are generated. The laws compare their interpretations. This is not an independent
full MTT syntax, arbitrary proof transport or a proof-relevant key model.
Forbidden ordinary-coordinate access is excluded for every function on the
restricted carrier, including generated substitutions.

The two native parameter changes complement the Boolean and select the largest
index of the new fiber. Their exact selected pair is preserved by the comparison;
this is not preservation of every old index across unequal fibers. Key extension,
by contrast, retains every supplied index. Separating these claims avoids turning
a selected-client test into an unproved equivalence of contexts.

## A precise internal partial-map boundary

The [assembly classifier](assembly-partial-map-classifier.md) advances #371 from
its program-lift prerequisite to P1. It classifies inherited-realizer domains
whose membership is uniformly semidecidable on all realizers. The construction
supplies tracked partial maps, both classification directions, exact round trips,
pullback universal properties, and stability under admitted reindexing. Mathlib
pullback interfaces expose those constructions to later categorical work.

The classifier generates delayed programs; its generating tracker does not
decide whether those programs halt. A halting-domain client retains the original
selected index. An indiscrete Boolean example rejects an arbitrary proper
subobject and an untracked distinguishing function. These controls keep the
admissibility boundary substantive. The independent zero-input and diagonal-input
presentations are related by actual tracked equivalences, without identifying
program codes or running times.

#386 still owes multiplication, monad laws and slice comparisons. #387–#389
still owe iteration, completeness and the selected solver. The classifier neither
constructs an effective topos nor validates every internal proposition from the
source paper. The full #371 provider remains open.

## Selection bias and continuing assessment

Keep logical admissibility, selected observations and operational behavior as
separate contracts. Check that restrictions used to obtain a bounded result do
not disappear when summarizing it. A proof-irrelevant truth observation is valid
when that is the client's requirement; it does not recover a prior selection.
Likewise, preserving a selected record does not validate a physical adapter.

Adopt the checked local constructions at their stated contracts: existing
indexed certificates suffice for the cursor client; restricted split families
supply the tested modal comprehension; retained operation state supports the
protected measurement; and saturated semidecidable domains supply the partial-map
classifier. Defer arbitrary dependent relators, full MTT, physical adapter
adequacy and SDT selection to their distinct obligations. The controls reject
invalid translations within these contracts, not the source traditions as a whole.

The client sample favors finite syntax and exact selected outputs. That choice
can understate the usefulness of truth-valued support or coarser behavioral
observations, and overstate author benefit when model-construction costs are
excluded. The delivery pole's weaker observation is a useful counterexample:
its failure to select the first answer does not invalidate its logical adequacy.
Conversely, the native comparisons remain short on several fixtures; the checked
shared laws do not establish lower total author effort. No matched productivity
measurement was performed in this wave.

A shared blind spot is uniformity beyond the displayed samples. Finite-fiber
checks do not construct an internal dependent family, and agreement of native
and interpreted event histories does not independently validate an adapter.
The all-context completeness contract and the explicit physical-correspondence
premises address those two different obligations without treating either as
already proved. Broader exploration remains with #235/#289/#290 and the named
operational owners; these results do not require a new mandatory representation.

The internal SDT provider remains open through #386–#389. Ordinary clients retain
their native interfaces. These tasks test specific missing capabilities rather
than requiring every tradition to pass through the existing compiler representation.
#107 stays paused, #108 remains unselected, and #260 inactive. Unanswered important
architectural questions block their dependent work.
