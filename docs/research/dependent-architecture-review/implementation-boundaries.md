# Dependent architecture: implementation and interface challenge

Independent assessment against `d108142f9b5875437c49441a4b45b3ad4988e5e5`, before reading the other reviewers' assessments. The proposed separation of author requirements, judgment interpretation, model construction, and setting changes is useful only if it removes concrete coupling and assigns dischargeable obligations. Much of it already exists. Renaming the present records would leave the main obstruction intact. A substantial replacement investigation is warranted, without assuming either that the existing foundations are unsound or that they must survive.

The [external memo](../dependent-coherence-discovery/292-external-memo.md) supplies the capability to retain: dependent transport, its composition witness, the dependent pair containing both, two substitutions, dependent action on a higher identity, and observable path and second-identity data. The [#292 report](../dependent-coherence-comparison.md) establishes only portions of this capability. Preserving it does not require preserving today's raw syntax, annotation representation, or theorem names.

## Where the current boundaries hold and fail

[`StructuralModel`](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/d108142f9b5875437c49441a4b45b3ad4988e5e5/SquareKit/Internal/PathTransport/Model.lean#L7) already contains category laws, type substitution laws, heterogeneous term substitution laws, and comprehension's projection, variable, and eta equations. [`ContextualModel`](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/d108142f9b5875437c49441a4b45b3ad4988e5e5/SquareKit/Internal/PathTransport/ContextualModel.lean#L112) derives pairing extensionality, lifted substitutions, path-context comparisons, telescope squares, and branch maps. This is positive architectural evidence: substantial dependent bookkeeping is constructed from reusable laws.

However, the structural record also requires `base`, its dependent `fiber`, `identity`, and `reflexivity`. These are particular signature and logical capabilities, mixed with context infrastructure. [`Model`](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/d108142f9b5875437c49441a4b45b3ad4988e5e5/SquareKit/Internal/PathTransport/ContextualModel.lean#L339) then requires contextual J over arbitrary finite dependent tails, its beta law, and its canonical substitution law. Every [`CtxCode`, `TyCode`, and `TmCode`](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/d108142f9b5875437c49441a4b45b3ad4988e5e5/SquareKit/Internal/PathTransport/Typed.lean#L12) quantifies over this entire model. Even a structural argument's annotations therefore depend on the whole supported theory. The interface cannot express that the argument needs only comprehension, or swap primitive signatures independently of its structural model.

[`TyDeriv` and `TmDeriv`](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/d108142f9b5875437c49441a4b45b3ad4988e5e5/SquareKit/Internal/PathTransport/Typed.lean#L213) index raw judgments by those semantic functions. [`Motive` and `PathCommand`](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/d108142f9b5875437c49441a4b45b3ad4988e5e5/SquareKit/Internal/PathTransport/Authoring.lean#L29) retain both representations and their derivations. This buys explicit soundness: [`interpretTmChecked`](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/d108142f9b5875437c49441a4b45b3ad4988e5e5/SquareKit/Internal/PathTransport/Typed.lean#L749) recursively interprets admitted rules and proves agreement with their annotations. It is not merely evaluation of an arbitrary host function. But it also exposes presentation choices in judgment types, before interpretation is selected.

That coupling explains the precise [#107 obstruction](../dependent-congruence-repair.md#remaining-obstruction-and-alternatives). Substitution of an existing concatenation yields J reindexed through an endpoint composite. Fresh concatenation after substitution builds another telescope, motive, and branch. `j_sub` covers the canonical path-telescope map; it does not directly identify these presentations. [`pairingConverted` and `substitutionCongrAt`](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/d108142f9b5875437c49441a4b45b3ad4988e5e5/SquareKit/Internal/PathTransport/StructuralCongruence.lean#L10) still request raw and semantic annotation alignments. A new `Interpretation` record with a field asserting this comparison would merely relocate the work unless a general construction supplies it.

## Missing laws, stronger assumptions, and counterexamples

The checked [`MissingTerminal.model`](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/d108142f9b5875437c49441a4b45b3ad4988e5e5/Examples/LocalUniversesBoundary.lean#L19) satisfies the entire interface on the discrete category of Booleans. No context receives a substitution from every context. Thus terminality genuinely cannot be derived; `empty` is only a distinguished context. This does not refute the existing fragment's soundness: its substitution constructors do not advertise arbitrary empty substitutions. Nor does it prove failure in the subcategory reachable from `empty`. Terminality belongs in an explicitly stronger structural capability if the admitted judgments or construction theorem need it.

Pairing naturality and identity/composition laws for lifted substitutions are plausible derivations from existing comprehension and substitution equations, using `pair_ext`. The exact fresh-command comparison remains unproved; it needs a typed factorization and compatible telescope, motive, and branch comparisons. Conversely, full comprehension with a specified category of family morphisms, categorical dependent exponentials, and additional type constructors are not supplied by naming the current record a CwF. The old `TyDeriv` has no Sigma constructor. Context extension alone does not demonstrate interpretation of the memo's Sigma-typed pair.

[`PresentationFailure`](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/d108142f9b5875437c49441a4b45b3ad4988e5e5/Examples/LocalUniverses.lean#L133) checks that delayed and fresh presentations decode to the same family while their records differ. It defeats reflection from decoded equality to presentation equality. It is not a proof that #107's particular comparison is impossible.

The concrete [`fullModel`](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/d108142f9b5875437c49441a4b45b3ad4988e5e5/SquareKit/Internal/PathTransport/GroupoidSoundness.lean#L269) actually proves contextual J substitution. Its general semantic families are groupoid-valued functors; its primitive `B` is set-valued. [`identityFamily`](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/d108142f9b5875437c49441a4b45b3ad4988e5e5/SquareKit/Internal/PathTransport/SplitFamily.lean#L159) makes identity fibers discrete. This retains first-level path distinctions but cannot supply the memo's nontrivial second homotopy observation. That restriction belongs to this instance, not automatically to `Model`: host `HEq` in substitution laws does not identify object-language identity with host equality. Separate algebraic two-loop examples do not establish a dependent higher model connected to this syntax.

## What a replacement should separate

A candidate should separate context/comprehension structure, primitive signature interpretations, and constructor capabilities such as Id and Sigma. Each constructor must state formation, introduction, elimination, computation, and substitution requirements. Its dependencies on earlier constructors should be explicit. An argument should inhabit a specified admitted fragment, whose interpretation theorem is derived structurally. A function polymorphic over records alone does not establish that theorem or a parametricity principle.

The hypothesis that additional laws should accompany additional structure needs qualification. Some laws are consequences to prove once, such as pairing naturality; adding them as independent assumptions would obscure the actual requirements. Some belong with newly offered operations, such as Sigma computation and stability. Others restrict the existing signature without introducing operations: requiring every identity fiber to have at most one section would select a narrower class of models. Such a restriction needs a named capability and must remain optional for arguments retaining path distinctions. Terminality can likewise be stated as a universal property of the existing `empty`, although an interface may expose its unique substitution as an operation. Neither fewer fields nor more layers alone establishes greater generality.

Keep model construction behind that boundary. A direct strict model may supply its laws without categorical strictification. The local-universes route requires full comprehension, LF, and the constructor-specific weak stability of [Lumsdaine–Warren, Theorem 3.4.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1411.1736); those hypotheses need constructions for the chosen setting. The current strict groupoid instance does not gain new established substitution laws merely by adding this route. A natural-model presentation packages comprehension through a representable presheaf transformation, but intensional identity still needs the lifting structure of [Awodey, Proposition 20](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1406.3219). Changing notation cannot discharge it. A chosen universe requires represented families, chosen pullbacks, and constructor structures; [Kapulkin–Lumsdaine, §§1.3–1.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.2851) provide a construction, not universal coverage of arbitrary families.

Changes of setting need maps preserving the relevant operations, dependent types, and selected witnesses, with identity and composition laws. Changes of presentation additionally need specified comparisons and independence from admitted choices. These may share infrastructure, but they answer different questions. Two separately interpreted models need not have a preservation map between them. A truncated instance can be legitimate while failing a particular observation requirement.

Strict type equations require their own interpretation evidence. Lumsdaine–Warren's §4.1 extension heuristic excludes conclusions asserting type equality. An extensible capability registry cannot promise arbitrary new equations merely because its existing constructors were transferred successfully.

## Prioritized investigation and acceptance

1. **Audit the minimum contract.** Classify every existing field and needed comparison as derived, independent, specialized, or unresolved. Construct or refute candidate missing laws before strengthening the record. Include terminality, pairing/lift composition, signature separation, generalized J, Sigma, and model morphisms. Acceptance requires proofs or explicit countermodels where claimed, and precise residual obligations elsewhere. Do not put LF into every author's requirements.

2. **Compare repair and replacement on one fixed argument.** Use the memo's transport witness and the already exposed concatenation case. Compare reusable presentation comparisons, fixed open templates, and a fresh judgment representation whose indices do not quantify over the complete semantic model. Permit replacing syntax, equality judgments, and the semantic foundation. The replacement must construct dependent argument environments and prove interpretation/substitution compatibility for its admitted rules. Acceptance requires two substitutions of the value, its dependent witness, and `apd_η(h)`, with no author-supplied model-code alignment. A postulated coherence field fails this test.

3. **Connect semantic capability.** Establish the nonconstant transport observation in a concrete instance and then the higher observation with `B(y)=Id_A(x,y)` and nontrivial `h`. A direct, local-universes, or chosen-universe construction may win. The higher phase can require substantial model development; a bounded initial experiment must name that remaining work rather than claim success from native equality or disconnected two-loop examples. Retain substitution into the observation and transported witness types. No object-language sphere constructor is required merely to supply a suitable semantic parameter.

4. **Test extension and reuse before selection.** Add one constructor depending on existing structure and derive its stability without revisiting ordinary arguments. Compare two presentations through explicit composable preservation maps. Record remaining author obligations and have independent authors apply the frozen candidate; reserve #108's unexposed theorem for its existing gate. Repair wins if it supplies the same capability economically. Layering wins if distinct construction routes reuse one judgment contract. Replacement wins if it removes recurring coupling while retaining the mathematical observations. Failure of these tests may justify another foundation, not weaker acceptance criteria.
