# Semantic traditions and their connections to the library

Assessment of checkout `ab51385a10d1936b55e48f18d791b3cfe2af91e1`, with GitHub issue states read on 2026-11-10. This is a source and research-record assessment, not a fresh Lean build or a literature survey. Historical wave reports must be read with their status corrections: the internal SDT provider, for example, is complete at its bounded scope. [Current reconciliation](workstream-status-reconciliation.md).

The investigations were intended to challenge the architecture, including its choice of observations and judgments. They were not limited to finding adapters for the current compiler. The discovery reports explicitly acknowledge incomplete literature coverage and selection bias. Some experiments construct an independent model before an incumbent consumer exists; others test whether existing machinery already suffices. Independence is therefore a research choice, not evidence that connection is mathematically impossible. [External comparison](external-language-comparison.md), [coverage map](semantic-traditions-coverage.md), [first-wave decision checks](semantic-traditions-first-wave.md), [#289](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/289).

## Source integration and mathematical reuse

A recursive import scan of root Lean files and `SquareKit/`, `Applications/`, and `Examples/` found 2,256 modules. All 2,255 other than `ProofAudit` are reachable from `SquareApplications`; the audit is a separate verification module. The count includes the entrypoint itself. The scan resolves dotted local module names and follows lines beginning with `import`; it measures source reachability, not compilation success. The [Lake configuration](../../lakefile.toml) and [public entrypoint](../../SquareApplications.lean) establish the build integration. The [README](../../README.md#build-and-audit) specifies separate validation for isolated `experiments/` and archived material.

Thus most semantic experiments are not isolated projects. They share the integrated build and proof standard. Their mathematical connections vary:

| Kind of connection | Evidence at this checkout |
|---|---|
| Existing compiler machinery directly reused | [RelationalTransfer](../../Examples/RelationalTransfer.lean) registers FIFO and interval clients with existing compiler interpretations. [IndexedRelationalTransfer](../../Examples/IndexedRelationalTransfer.lean) uses dependent-operation local certificates for changing capacities and selected cursor data. These are actual reuse, not just imports into the entrypoint. |
| A new result used across domains | [Numerical.Error](../../SquareKit/Numerical/Error.lean) supplies sensitivity-aware error composition; both [NumericalError](../../Examples/NumericalError.lean) and [CheckedMotion](../../Applications/Orbifold/CheckedMotion.lean) import it. This connects interval examples and numerical motion without equating enclosure, metric error and execution cost. |
| Independent model with a local development chain | [QuasiBorel](../../Applications/Probability/QuasiBorel.lean) constructs its own spaces and morphisms from Mathlib. Its project importer is [RandomFunction](../../Applications/Probability/RandomFunction.lean), which is then used by [SamplerSubstitution](../../Applications/Probability/SamplerSubstitution.lean) and its example. It is not another target of the original categorical compiler. |
| Bounded alternative with few external consumers | [NominalBodies](../../SquareKit/Internal/NominalBodies.lean) is imported only by its example; its predecessor [NominalTelescope](../../SquareKit/Internal/NominalTelescope.lean) is also used by that extension. [OpenSystems.Wiring](../../Applications/OpenSystems/Wiring.lean) is directly imported only by its example, which later approximate-wiring examples reuse. This demonstrates a local theorem chain, not broad application adoption. |
| New semantics connected to existing semantic infrastructure | [ModalHigher.Family](../../SquareKit/Internal/ModalHigher/Family.lean), [Total](../../SquareKit/Internal/ModalHigher/Total.lean) and [Keys](../../SquareKit/Internal/ModalHigher/Keys.lean) use `DoubleDelooping`, `TwoFamily` and higher reindexing. [HornDerivations](../../SquareKit/HornDerivations.lean) adds selected derivations to [HornSupport](../../SquareKit/HornSupport.lean). |
| A substantial independent categorical model | The [assembly provider](internal-selected-certificate-solver.md) constructs tracked maps, partial maps, lifting, iteration, complete families and a selected solver. Its categorical interfaces and external comparison connect it to the rest of the mathematics; it does not replace every ordinary program interface. |

These importer statements are direct edges, inspected in the Lean source; transitive reuse can be broader. An entrypoint import alone does not prove a semantic adapter, runtime integration or broad adoption. In particular, [approximate wiring](approximate-wiring-experiment.md) establishes a finite discrete comparison but supplies no FRP or numerical-motion adapter. The open [#430](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/430) owns the latter connection.

## What the trials conclude

The evidence supports retaining ordinary interfaces for several tested clients. Existing compiler registrations sufficed for FIFO/interval transfer; indexed operation maps sufficed for the cursor; the native handler remains the ordinary finite-query interface. Those are bounded conclusions about specific obligations. [Second wave](semantic-traditions-second-wave.md), [fourth wave](semantic-traditions-fourth-wave.md), [fifth wave](semantic-traditions-fifth-wave.md).

It does not support the stronger conclusion that the investigations demonstrated no improvements. They produced new capabilities, including random-function exponentials and law substitution, tracked internal partial computation, generated modal-access restrictions, selected nominal substitution, and strategy interaction/hiding. Numerical-error composition also became a shared result used by actual motion code. These improvements need not replace the square compiler to count as useful mathematics. The linked code and reports above specify their limits.

There was also a local architectural adoption: #315 introduced a tested dependent judgment-interpretation boundary whose source judgments no longer require universal semantic-code indices. It retained the earlier annotated clients, and the assessment recommends that boundary without claiming a universal migration or overall author-effort win. This is a change to how part of the library is organized, not merely an added example. [Dependent architectural reassessment](dependent-architecture-reassessment.md), [#289 implementation evidence](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/289).

Nor did the trials establish that the incumbent architecture is globally superior. Several syntheses report no matched productivity measurement; the author-interface trial found both routes viable on its selected fragment. Many fixtures favor finite syntax and exact selected outputs, which can undervalue truth-oriented support and intentionally coarser observations. No universal migration follows, but no whole-design-space ranking follows either. [Dependent author trial](dependent-author-interface-trial.md), [first-wave bias analysis](semantic-traditions-first-wave.md), [fifth-wave disposition](semantic-traditions-fifth-wave.md). The open [#235](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/235) explicitly retains architecture evolution and recovery of useful existing specializations.

## Foundation and Felix

The stable architectural principle separates operations, semantic equivalence and laws, then expresses structure-preserving interpretations and transports proofs under stated assumptions. [Category](../../SquareKit/Category.lean), [CategoryMap](../../SquareKit/CategoryMap.lean), [Comma](../../SquareKit/Comma.lean), [Squares](../../Squares.lean) and [Compiler](../../Compiler.lean) implement that principle. Exact commutation and relational preservation are distinct contracts; the dependent, modal, probabilistic and tracked models add further contracts rather than obtaining all their semantics from one equality square. [Preservation inventory](preservation-contract-inventory.md).

This inherits Felix's categorical compilation and law-transport architecture. The [provenance record](../SOURCES.md) pins Felix, and the [implementation record](felix-parity-implementation.md) connects local constructions to its raw categories, equivalence, laws, homomorphisms, comma construction and `MakeLawful`. Local closed-law transport completes a construction left as a CartesianClosed TODO upstream; relational squares and the later semantic developments extend the scope. Mathlib integration and Lean's encoding also differ from Felix's Agda implementation. This is architectural inheritance with substantial extensions, not a dependency on Felix or a completed port of all its work. [#8](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/8) and the matched elegance/caller-effort comparison [#25](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/25) remain open.
