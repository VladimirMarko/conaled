## What the constructions establish

Modal access now has a generated language whose variable rule requires an
actual key and whose admissible substitutions preserve that requirement.
Accessible environments carry values rather than a policy bit. The native
restricted-context function remains the smaller interface for the fixed client;
the generated route additionally derives substitution closure and rejects every
generated ordinary-variable observer under the tested locks. Identity families
vary with their endpoints, but the selected diagonal witness is proof irrelevant.
[#384](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/384) tests the omitted
context extension where a later declaration's type depends on an earlier value.
Thin reachability cannot settle comparisons between distinct parallel keys.

The assembly milestone separates an underlying function from evidence that it
is an admitted internal morphism. Its tracker must work on every realizer of an
input. Natural-number arrows coincide with computable functions. Program-index
lifting constructs bottom, maps and a unit without deciding halting. These are
concrete prerequisites; transporting the independently specified certificate
does not establish that an internal fixed-point selector produces it.
[#371](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/371) remains open, with
#385–#389 owning the classifier, monad, iteration/extension, complete family and
selected-client comparison. Their dependency edges retain the missing work.

The wiring construction proves that composition preserves admissibility of the
finite port bijections, then derives system substitution laws. The two product
hierarchies agree with the independent native vector field and readout through
the stated coordinate isomorphisms and their derivatives. This is a full-system
structural theorem, not equality of one final scalar observation. The second
port permutation exercises actual wiring, but the chosen component sums its
inputs symmetrically; it is not evidence that an uncompensated swap changes its
dynamics. The generic composition theorem has a broader system parameter.

The C4 presentation proves local diffeomorphism properties for its source and
target and properness of the actual anchor. Its arrow comparison retains labels
and isotropy and connects to the existing canonical recovery comparison. Generic
label laws and a noncommutative control prevent the finite cyclic example from
hiding composition-order errors. None of this identifies action-groupoid arrows
with trajectories or reduces the groupoid to its coarse orbit set.

Horn support now separates truth of a formula from the selected derivation
used to establish an atom. Implication quantifies over future bases inside an
explicit permitted basis. Enlarging that permitted basis can invalidate an
implication which held vacuously before, even when the current base and formula
stay unchanged. This makes the ambient assumption policy part of the contract.

The optional labelled layer reuses the existing finite derivation and theory-map
machinery. Compatible rule additions copy the selected tree and its trace;
choosing a shortest proof again can change that trace. A concrete rule replacement
has a checked translation through local certificates, but this does not establish
formula transport for arbitrary basis changes. Erasing a tree to support cannot
recover every original selection; it can still permit choosing some proof.

## Changes to the decision audit

Review found a planning defect in the SDT prerequisite graph even though the
formal code passed. The concrete state algebra was scheduled before construction
of its state family, which already depended on the earlier node. The corrected
graph puts generic lifting-monad laws first and the concrete algebra after the
family. Check dependency graphs as well as Lean claims: a build cannot detect
an impossible sequence of future theorem obligations.

Symmetric examples also need careful interpretation. C4 commutativity and a
symmetric sum can conceal an ordering or permutation mistake in a concrete
calculation. Inspect the general theorem and use a discriminating control when
the claimed distinction needs one. The proper-etale comparison includes such a
noncommutative control; the wiring report does not overclaim its symmetric swap.
