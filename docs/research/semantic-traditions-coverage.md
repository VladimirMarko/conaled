The local-universes hypothesis has a concrete limit. Lumsdaine–Warren,
Definition 3.1.3 and Theorem 3.4.1 require a full comprehension category with
finite products and specified dependent exponentials (LF), plus weak stability
of the requested constructors. Section 4.1 excludes general type-equality rules
from the lifting; their local-universe presentations can differ. Thus admitting
new conversion rules in #345 is not automatically compatible with applying this
coherence construction. This is a reason to inspect the actual presentation,
not to discard the construction. [Primary source, pp. 15, 19, 32–33](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1411.1736v2).

## Four deferred probes retain concrete acceptance tests

These are residual questions from #301, not reopened completed experiments.
Each has an open coordinator and a condition for creating an implementation
child. The conditions preserve the questions without requiring four speculative
infrastructure projects now.

**Nominal binding, #289/#290.** Gabbay–Pitts §5 proves an initial-algebra account
of alpha classes and freshness-conditioned recursion in FM sets. It does not
supply this project's dependent judgment interpretation. Promote when an actual
client repeats binder/freshness obligations after ordinary author-interface
repair. Compare substitution beneath two binders, free-variable support and a
selected dependent payload against an independent interpretation. Reject a
bound-name observer: `[a]a=[b]b` cannot return the respective distinct names.
Two binder renamings must preserve meaning, not merely a raw syntax encoding.
No theorem is selected or prepared for #108, and #107 remains paused.
[Gabbay–Pitts, Theorem 5.1, Lemma 5.4, Theorem 5.5](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~amp12/papers/newaas/newaas.pdf).

**Proof-theoretic support, #290.** A basis is a class of allowed atomic systems;
a base extension must remain in that class. Gheorghiu–Pym Definitions 2.4–2.6
therefore do not mean arbitrary extension of one fixed model. The implication
support clause quantifies over all permitted extensions. The matching
soundness/completeness results depend on the chosen basis. The re-read still
finds the displayed derived clause labelled disjunction with a conjunction on
its left and an incomplete premise; these displays are not an approved formal
specification. Before implementing, reconcile them with a corrected primary
version or derive the needed fragment from the unambiguous primitive clauses.
Promote for a client requiring support under two atomic-base extensions and an
independent selected-witness bridge. Reject basis-independent logic and
truth-only substitutes for that selected witness. The existence of an
inferential definition of meaning does not supply this bridge.
[Primary manuscript, Definitions 2.4–2.6, Figure 2 and subsequent displays,
Theorems 3.7/3.11](https://www.cantab.net/users/david.pym/Proof_theoretic_Semantics_for_Second_order_Logic.pdf).

The subsequent [#377 construction](atomic-base-support-construction.md) completes
the atoms/implication probe on paper and warrants a bounded formal successor.
It derives persistence, introduction/application, both strict base extensions
and a labelled-tree inclusion bridge. Selected-trace recovery from support
alone fails, while extension-quantified support remains independently useful.
The earlier client-and-witness promotion condition above therefore applies to
selected-witness clients, not to every use of inferential support. No full
second-order implementation is claimed.

**Classical realizability, #290 with #196.** #296 tested state/exception policies;
it did not construct a classical realizability algebra, pole or interpretation
of a classical principle. Krivine's adequacy theorem is relative to these data.
Promote when a client selects an uncovered principle (for example Peirce's law
with its control realizer) and a nontrivial observation/pole contract. Require
an arithmetic witness with its dependent certificate, a change of continuation
interpretation, and a proof that the chosen observation survives that change.
Reject the universal pole, which is backward closed but makes realizability
vacuous for this purpose. A syntactic realizer or adequacy alone does not prove
selected-output recovery or execution termination.
[Krivine, §1, Theorem 1.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1005.2395).

The subsequent [#378 construction](classical-control-selected-search.md) supplies
the bounded control/pole experiment on paper. It proves implicational adequacy
and Peirce for a lambda/control machine, constructs a proper delivery pole and
proves coherence for pure proof-like terms. A separate traversal invariant and
closure-to-frame simulation preserve first-even certificates; later-even output
passes the weaker pole and fails selection. Bounded formalization can proceed
without an incumbent consumer. This is not full classical arithmetic extraction.

**Relational hiding, #27/#36 with #348.** Baez–Pollard's black-boxing theorem
maps polynomial open dynamical systems to semialgebraic steady-state relations;
composition existentially hides internal boundary data. #300 compared wiring
of full vector fields and readouts and did not instantiate this theorem.
Promote for a consumer requiring equality of externally observable steady-state
relations. Compare two polynomial systems and their composite relation, then
strengthen the contract to retain a selected internal solution and certificate.
The closed field `v(x)=x(x−1)` has solutions 0 and 1 with the same empty
boundary tuple, so that observation cannot recover the selection. The example
is a paper obstruction, not a solver or trajectory theorem. #348's general
relational boundary does not by itself prove this analytic instance.
[Baez–Pollard, Definition 22, Theorem 23](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1704.02051).

## Adjoint categorical logic: witnesses and base change

Lawvere's account relates substitution and quantification through adjunctions.
Its commentary distinguishes slice objects carrying proof bundles from a poset
reflection recording the existence of a deduction, and further from subobject
truth. These collapses need not be isomorphisms. For locally cartesian closed
settings, dependent products can be understood as right adjoints to pullback;
composition in slices supplies dependent sums. These are hypotheses on the
setting, not consequences of declaring a quantifier in syntax.
[Lawvere, commentary pp. 1–2 and §3](https://tac.mta.ca/tac/reprints/articles/16/tr16.pdf).

**Paper client.** Let `A(n)=Fin(n+2)`, with chosen element `s(n)=n`. This specifies
both the changing fiber and the selected value. Pullback along `f(k)=k+1` and
then `g(j)=2*j` selects `2*j+1 : Fin(2*j+3)`, exactly as pullback along `f∘g`.
Package the witness as a dependent pair and retain its first projection as an
observation. Replacing it by `Nonempty (A(n))` loses the distinction between
`0` and `1` in `A(0)`. A reconstruction preserving each original choice would
have to return both from the same proposition. Classical choice can choose an
inhabitant; it cannot invert that loss of the selected input.

This is already expressible by native families. The experiment should determine
which general sum/reindexing laws derive the calculation and which setup costs
remain. It must use the slice sum/pullback adjunction and compare chosen
presentations, rather than postulate the final selected-value equation.
Substitution alone, Sigma existence, Pi existence and invertible base change
are separate capabilities. A Sigma client must not inherit all Pi hypotheses.

There is more local support than an isolated mate lemma:

- [`AdjointComparison`](../../SquareKit/AdjointComparison.lean) proves curry,
  uncurry and mate compatibility under canonical changes of right adjoint.
- [`DependentBaseChange`](../../SquareKit/DependentBaseChange.lean) constructs
  the sum/pullback comparison for an actual pullback square and proves
  `WithAdjunction.mate_isIso` using the admitted right adjoints.
- [`SheafDependentProduct.baseChange_comparison`](../../SquareKit/SheafDependentProduct.lean)
  already consumes `AdjointComparison.mate`; it relates the constructed sheaf
  dependent product to other chosen right adjoints.

Thus the new question is the reusable witness/quantifier client and its
preservation requirements. It is not whether mates exist in the codebase.
A mate gives a canonical comparison, not automatic invertibility in arbitrary
situations. For a paper failure control, take a commuting square with upper-left
set empty and its other three sets singleton. It is not a pullback. Base change
of the inhabited family along the bottom identity is inhabited, whereas the
sum from the empty upper-left fiber is empty. No proposed sum comparison is
invertible. The existing theorem's `IsPullback` premise excludes this case.

[#351](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/351) owns the bounded
checked experiment under #289/#290, coordinated with
#343/#349: selected sums through two substitutions, change of chosen adjoints,
comparison to existential truth, the empty-square control and an obligation
ledger against native calculations. Units/counits and their laws supply
substantive structure; opposite arrow directions alone do not supply an
adjunction or inverse compiler.

## Mode-based adjoint logic: resource use and compilation

Pruiksma–Chargin–Pfenning–Reed use a preorder of modes, with structural permissions
`σ(m) ⊆ {W,C}` monotone in `m≥k`, and require a conclusion at k to depend only
on hypotheses at modes above k. Exchange is admitted throughout. Their shifts
and cut-elimination results concern this specified propositional schema;
they do not cover arbitrary setting changes or arbitrary dependent types.
[Adjoint Logic, §§2–3](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~fp/papers/adjoint18b.pdf).

Jang et al. give natural deduction, a machine and algorithmic typing for the
mode discipline. Preservation/progress are separate from their conditional
claims about completed computations. In particular Theorems 19–20 concern
closed computations terminating at a purely positive type. Section 6 adds
recursive definitions; the report must not infer general normalization from
these results. Section 8 discusses dependent directions rather than providing
our required full dependent compiler model.
[Adjoint Natural Deduction, §§2–8, Theorems 15–20](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.01428v1).

**Paper discrimination.** Choose modes U≥L with `σ(U)={W,C}` and `σ(L)=∅`.
A tensor introduction using the same generic linear hypothesis in both branches
requires a forbidden context merge. Discarding that hypothesis to produce the
unit similarly requires missing weakening. Both operations are available at U.
The test concerns arbitrary atomic A with no supplied copying or consuming
primitive; some particular inhabited types can admit specialized maps. Additive
conjunction has a different context rule and must not be used to bypass this
tensor test.

An independent semantic check can use a resource-sensitive category at L. For
example, in the symmetric monoidal closed poset of integers ordered by ≤ with
tensor addition and unit 0, implication is subtraction. For atomic object −1,
the proposed generic diagonal would require `−1≤−2`, which is false; for object
1, generic discard requires `1≤0`, also false. These are paper controls for
linear structure, not a model of the entire two-mode calculus: a full mixed-mode
interpretation must additionally construct suitable U structure and the shift
adjunction. Ordinary sets with all functions can erase this distinction and
are insufficient as the only rejection evidence.

The promoted compiler task should freeze a legal transfer/consumption client,
its independent selected result and resource meaning, and a supported fragment
with genuine adjoint shifts. Compare direct resource contracts with the mode
layer. Derive preservation for arbitrary fragment constructions from local
interpretations and retain identity/two-stage composition; reject forbidden
copying, discarding and a nonmonotone permission policy. Keep compile correctness,
source metatheory and runtime behavior separate. A small shift capability can
extend a simple per-mode interface; the experiment need not demand a maximal
modal/quantifier package from every caller.

This differs from #297's two-lock MTT problem. Its access 2-cells and dependent
right adjoints cannot be substituted for ADJ's resource context merge or machine
proofs. Conversely the resource calculus does not supply dependent lock
substitution or a nontrivial higher identity interpretation. [#352](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/352) owns this successor
under #36/#290, with #343/#349 and #196 coordinating its boundaries.

## Other qualified trails and prioritization

The first-pass [computational survey](external-computational-semantics.md) also
retains games, separation/resource semantics, matching logic and quantitative
dependent syntax. Their appearance in a survey is not a completed applicability
test. Keep these concrete deferred tasks under the existing open programmes:

- #196/#36: if interaction observations exceed returned values, compare a game
  strategy with an independent trace meaning; reject equating strategies solely
  from equal output distributions. Ground adequacy alone is insufficient for
  all higher-order contexts.
- #196/#193: if a client needs local ownership or interference, supply its
  separating resource algebra and frame/interaction hypotheses. A scalar cost
  monoid is not a proof of separation or a safe physical rollback contract.
- #288/#290: if matching-logic patterns are selected as an admission boundary,
  compare satisfaction and rule interpretation on one extension; explicitly
  retain proof/witness data that set membership forgets.
- #193/#289: if exact usage multiplicities, rather than only permissions W/C,
  are required, compare quantitative dependent syntax with the mode experiment.
  Require substitution of grades and dependent payloads; successful linear
  permission checking is not already that theorem.

These are triggers, not automatic implementations. Probability-sheaf/internal
valuation variants remain with #36 under the distinct observations in
[the probability comparison](probability-model-comparison.md); neither #298's
ordinary joint-law case nor #347's SDT provider settles them.

Prioritize the two adjoint experiments because the owner requested their
compiler/library applicability and existing constructions provide concrete
reuse tests. Continue #343–#349 at their own scopes. Retain the four deferred
probes above until their named client or source-definition condition holds.
Broader foundations remain eligible to replace the current representation;
absence of an immediate adapter is not a rejection criterion. Any later
promotion may revise this order on mathematical or author-obligation evidence.

Validation for this audit is relative-link checking, whitespace checking,
source-locator inspection and reconciliation against the tracker snapshot.
