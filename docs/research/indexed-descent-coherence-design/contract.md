## Recommendation

Reuse the existing `inverseSquareAcross` proof family inside the public same-source specializations `inverseSquare_spec`, `inverseSquare_comp`, and `inverseSquare_canonical`. Preserve the existing `inverseSquare`, `normalizedSquare`, `inverseSquareOver`, and normalization/identity statements and their chosen isomorphisms. This removes duplicate equivalence arguments while maintaining direct refinement APIs. No new record or universal coherence interface is needed.

`inverseSquareOver` is definitionally equal to `inverseSquareAcross` at the same source category. The old `inverseSquare` is the identity-left-edge specialization, with `i ≪≫ E₁.functor.leftUnitor.symm` supplying the correctly typed square. A scratch file checks its component equality and derives the existing composition and canonical statements directly from Across. This is an adoption candidate with concrete proof reuse, not merely moving declarations to another file.

The two mathematical consumers are stack descent equivalences over covers and heterogeneous indexed-family equivalences over contexts. The latter already uses Across directly; the former uses Across for parameter change and currently repeats its proof for same-base refinement. Thus the same common argument already serves two independently motivated contracts, and specialization further removes duplication within the descent consumer. Whether moving the remaining ordinary-category declarations into `EquivalenceSquare` improves module boundaries is secondary; it is not itself evidence of a new shared obligation.

## Identity alternative

A possible ordinary lemma would take `E : A ≌ D`, endofunctors L,R, chosen `l : L ≅ 𝟭 A`, `r : R ≅ 𝟭 D`, and `i : E.functor ⋙ R ≅ L ⋙ E.functor`. From

`i.hom.app a ≫ E.functor.map (l.hom.app a) = r.hom.app (E.functor.obj a)`

it would derive

`E.inverse.map (r.inv.app d) ≫ (inverseSquareAcross E E R L i).hom.app d = l.inv.app (E.inverse.obj d)`.

This has two plausible exact consumers: the existing same-source identity theorem (L is identity) and `CoGrothendieckTransport.inverseNaturality_id` (L/R are selected identity-substitution functors). I have not checked the generic proof or both adapters. The present transport proof is a short counit-naturality argument and the stack specialization is also short. Keep both unless a checked probe shows that shared proof plus client setup improves maintenance. Do not justify it merely by the word “identity,” and do not replace selected mapId comparisons with strict identities.

## Existing Mathlib versus larger interface

The definitions already use Mathlib `Iso.isoCompInverse`, `Iso.inverseCompIso`, functor associators/unitors and `Equivalence` triangle/naturality laws. `EquivalenceSquare` supplies their component specification, composition and recovery equations. The checked specialization costs one identity functor and one left-unitor isomorphism per square, then simp; native callers need no new data. This is preferable for the observed duplication to a record of forward and inverse coherence fields, which would duplicate derived obligations. A larger pseudofunctor/displayed-category interface could support later broader results, but these consumers do not require it and this assessment does not establish a migration benefit.

An adjacent source search found `SmoothQuotientCoverPresentationSections`' private `inverseSquare` (line 19), used by `presentationInverseProjection` (line 77). Its formula is the symmetric Across construction with total and base equivalences; it keeps dependent matching sections and their projection. This is a real additional reuse candidate, but outside my primary lens. The source formula matches; I did not compile that migration. Its private `inverseProjection` already expresses a two-step Mathlib whiskering construction. Neither small definition alone warrants a new interface or expansion of #723.
