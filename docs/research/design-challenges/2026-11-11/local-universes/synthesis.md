## What would justify a broader strategy

A further groupoid experiment must use a varying dependent domain and nontrivial arrows. Require evaluation and a full natural representation, a constructed generic operation, the selected computation laws, and repeated substitution on arrow maps. An object-only candidate should fail a deliberate arrow-sensitive test. Preserve the weak-unit counterexamples and the existing distinction between equal decoded families and equal presentations.

The fixed-product weak-unit currying construction does not already represent every dependent telescope. A dependent-exponential or display-closure requirement must be exposed and proved at its actual universe levels. Parameter representability alone does not construct Π. The alternatives report separately discusses the primary paper's LF and pseudo-stable replacement hypotheses; the other lenses did not independently verify that paper. Those literature comparisons therefore remain attributed to that report, rather than three independently confirmed foundation theorems.
