# Foundations assessment of #602

Recommendation: retain the declared-domain construction as a paper-level research result, with the small clarifications below before publication. I found no separating obstruction or unsound step in its classifier, aligned Glue, universe composition, or finite comparison. The actual #602 contract permits a declared restricted domain and a native comparison on the finite overlap. It does not demand a global RU→U map. The proposed span meets that reading because each leg contains the original selected square as a whole face. Independent Standards/Spec review remains required; this report is a design assessment, not formal acceptance or a checked proof.

## Evidence supporting retention

The external dependency is appropriate. ABCFHL Theorem 2 (§3.2, PDF pp. 41–42) requires finite products, distinct endpoints, and Cof⊆Ωdec closed under interval equality, disjunction and interval quantification. Its strictification justification uses Orton–Pitts Theorem 6.3. Sections 2.11–2.12 (pp. 26–29) supply the incoherent Glue composition, its alignment, universe composition, ua and its transport comparison; §3.1.5 gives the structured classification rules. These are construction dependencies, not evidence for the candidate's new finite span. I checked the formulas in the [primary manuscript](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~rwh/papers/uniform/uniform.pdf), including the equivalence-record overlap in universe composition. The Agda development was not executed.

The candidate independently instantiates those requirements. Classical membership decision for every sieve does not make the internal interval decidable or collapse its paths. Pointwise external decisions need not define a complemented subpresheaf. In particular, the endpoint sieve can change from false to true under restriction; the strictification recipe handles exactly this transition through conjugation of the restriction map. Requiring a stable complement would be an erroneous extra assumption.

The universe sizes are consistent with an external hierarchy of types. A represented chart has a small category of elements. Its families range in Type u, hence their collection lies in Type(u+1); a selected composition has small indexing data once the underlying chart family is fixed. Arbitrarily large base contexts do not require enlarging each chart's fibers. Consequently J:N→U_u does not become ill-sized just because N itself lives one level higher. The u=0 caveat should remain explicit in a Lean port. The construction assumes the ambient universe hierarchy and does not construct it inside itself.

The chart decoder recovers the selected composition as well as elements: a represented box is evaluated in its generic extended cube, and the recovered operation is the original operation by its substitution law. I inspected `LocalFamily.code`, `code_reindex`, `decodeCodeIso`, `code_represented` and `decodeCode_filling` in `SquareKit/Internal/Cubical/SmallFamilyClassification.lean` (lines 31, 37, 63, 90 and 377). These support the proposed proof pattern, but are the existing D construction, not a checked Cartesian implementation.

## Record alignment and overlap checks

The Glue argument passes the two checks most likely to expose a hidden weakening.

First, the fiber extension prescribes the actual endpoint element, paired with its constant unglue path, on ψ∨(r=s). The two prescriptions agree on their intersection by the original box boundary. Correcting b′ with q must retain that same union as a constant tube. This protects the diagonal equation even when r=s is a proper subobject rather than a syntactic equality.

Second, χ=∀z.φ(p(z)) gives a partial *whole-line* T filling. Its intersection with the old tube agrees by T's boundary law, and its value on the source graph agrees by T's diagonal law. Enlarging the incoherent box by this tube forces its output to equal T's selected operation on χ. This is sufficient for equality of the restricted composition records: every line in the restricted context lies in φ at every coordinate. There is no inference from endpoint membership alone to χ.

The U composition's ψ and r=s branches agree as families with selected operations, because the U-box boundary is equality of codes, including those operations. Their equivalence structures also need to agree. The candidate deliberately transports the identity equivalence structure along the varying target, so diagonal composition recovers that exact identity structure on r=s. Merely knowing that the transport map is the identity would not suffice, and the candidate avoids that error.

On finite endpoints, decoding identifies the whole family with the constant finite carrier, so its equivalence family has a discrete presentation. This justifies lifting each whole comparison square to a common coe fiber with compatible path-to-e components. It does not imply that the universe's own square bodies are equal. Contractibility plus filling then extends the four prescribed boundary faces while retaining both square bodies. I inspected `HomotopyContractible.extend`, `extend_boundary`, and `extend_reindex` in `EquivalenceExtension.lean` (lines 50–71); they support this recipe in the existing model. The Cartesian versions remain paper derivations.

The accepted #600 report, sections “Chosen relative witness between H and H_new” and “Substitution and composition of the chosen comparison,” already distinguishes relative extension from equality of nerve images. Reusing its fixed Ω_R and constructing Ω_U independently preserves that distinction. A span through the specified T and the two decoder isomorphisms is enough for #602's overlap wording. It cannot be advertised later as a global functor transporting arbitrary RU interiors.

## Corrections and discriminating tests

No established mathematical defect found. Three clarifications would reduce implementation ambiguity without strengthening caller assumptions:

1. Add the code η law to the classifier discussion: coding the universal decoder pulled back along a:Γ→U recovers a, with its composition record. Derive it chartwise from a's naturality. This makes explicit the structured classifier property used when universe composition returns a pre-existing boundary code. The present argument contains the ingredients, so this is an omitted derivation rather than an obstruction.
2. Spell out the boundary-alignment construction for `aligned J(T)`: extend a partial section of Path_U(a,a) from the original square and the selected endpoint homotopies, producing a new square and a comparison to J(T). This makes visible where J(T) survives as a face before applying the contractible-fiber extension to H_U. The candidate currently compresses this step into “use the preceding endpoint two-paths to align.” Retain the original square and all endpoint adapters, as #600 does.
3. State that the resulting finite comparison is uniform on the substitution closure of the frozen client. Its composite-substitution equation follows by pulling back the entire fixed witness. It does not provide a fresh-choice naturality theorem over arbitrary varying finite equivalences or a pentagon. The manuscript already says most of this; preserve the limitation in the outcome statement.

Useful future checked tests are: a Glue line entering φ only at an endpoint, a diagonal r=s that is a proper face, and two distinct chosen composition records on the same underlying family. The last test should recover the chosen record on each boundary instead of identifying records because their decoded sets agree. For the span, test the face equations using the original H and T, not only their transports or boundary permutations. These checks discriminate the intended construction from weaker implementations; their absence is not evidence of impossibility.

## Higher consumer and acceptance boundary

The B(Z/2) example lies in the declared domain using the groupoid nerve filler. Its square relation with constant vertical sides forces equal horizontal arrows, so its two based loops are distinct up to relative homotopy despite its single vertex. The displayed central-automorphism formula gives the required homotopy naturally under vertex maps. Lifting it to complete equivalences requires the stated relative alignment in isEquiv; proof irrelevance of metatheoretic propositions alone would not replace that step. The coe/ua comparison then transfers the separation to universe two-paths. I found no dimension shift or vertex-only collapse in this argument. It remains a paper derivation.

Thus the mathematical acceptance items are met at research level, subject to making the compressed alignment step explicit and to the independent acceptance review. The stronger Ω filling hypothesis is a real restriction relative to formula-only Kan inputs, but it is disclosed and allowed by the issue's declared-domain wording. No claim about all formula-only families follows. The Z/2 consumer establishes that the admitted domain is already larger than finite local systems in a consequential way.
