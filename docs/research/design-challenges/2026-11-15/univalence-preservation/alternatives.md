## Concrete route and the alternatives

The recommended target is the host groupoid-shaped observation fragment with types as objects and bijections as arrows. For the bounded first implementation it can be expressed directly with `Equiv`, without first installing a categorical wrapper. The chosen comparison is actual decoded native transport followed by the constant-family decoder and closed-section observation. It is not merely renaming a carrier: a native path becomes a bijection, and a native two-path yields host equality of those bijections.

The existing self-loop comparison is already constructed in `Examples/FiniteUniverseLoops.lean:19–84`. `observedEquivalence` uses the actual `pathToEquivalence p`; `loopPermutation` extracts its chosen inverse through the fiber center. `permutationMap` recovers the whole transport map, while `classifyLoop` recovers a loop only through a native two-path. `twoPath_iff_permutation` precisely characterizes existence of a two-path. This is stronger evidence than a conjectured functor between models, but weaker than exact recovery of loop terms, contraction records, or two-path bodies. `DiscreteEquivalences.closedEquiv` explicitly calls itself a classification of section observations, not native equivalence witnesses.

A useful extension is constant-code paths between different host types, together with whole structured-package observations along the exact supplied `uaCode f E` line. `Examples/CubicalStructuredState.lean:191–227` already derives the carrier isomorphism from arbitrary supplied `E`, rather than replacing `E` by `ofIso`; lines 301–326 recover the source package, target conjugate package and transported section. This gives a concrete connection to the required six-field client. The proposed next theorem should relate this observed carrier bijection to the path-to-bijection route, then carry the entire `StatePackage` observation. That connecting theorem has not been checked in this assessment.

## Direct laws, a small interface, and a broad package

Use the same client for all three: two finite carrier changes, the selected native composite equivalence and its inverse, the actual comparison square, and the transported six-field package. Include Bool identity/negation and the Fin 3 order reversal as rejecting cases.

Direct laws currently have the clearest advantage. They can name the actual transport decoder and prove output preservation using existing theorems, without supplying an additional abstract univalence law. The full selected source records remain arguments; the target contract explicitly says which observations are preserved. This provides an independently usable bridge even if the broader architecture changes.

A small interface becomes useful when another route repeats the same continuation proof. It should separate operations/maps from their compatibility and from reflection. For the bounded fragment, expose a carrier observation, an arrow observation with its application square, and package observation compatibility. Prove observed identity, composition and inverse from actual transport comparisons and discreteness when possible. Do not require authors to supply these consequences again. If the interface promises whole comparison preservation, it additionally needs a target comparison type and comparison map; a Prop-valued equation cannot silently stand in for the source square body.

A broad package could parameterize contexts, comprehension, admitted universes, paths, dependent constructors and selected filling, then derive `ua` preservation from preservation of the construction. That could remove repeated proofs across genuinely different models and accommodate capabilities the finite route cannot express. Its costs are substantial dependent coherence and a choice of strict equality versus specified comparison at each operation. Requiring strict equality of all chosen fillers would exclude useful weak comparisons; allowing an unspecified comparison with no coherence would fail to recover the selected clients. Which equations are derivable must be established from an actual constructor, not guessed from a maximal field list.

#446 is the relevant architectural precedent: its primitive natural isomorphism and fiber square derive whole generated section and selected counit comparisons. It does not ask the caller to assume the final J-preservation theorem. `docs/research/signature-cylinder-comparison.md` identifies the declarations and the nonfaithful example. Its ordinary groupoid semantics does not supply a cubical univalence adapter. The lesson is to investigate derivation from primitive compatibility and retain selected components, not to impose groupoid faithfulness everywhere.

#490/#492 contribute the distinction between mathematical admission, evidence encoding and source/artifact association. Their reports do not prove a cubical interpretation and do not select a production interface. MMT/theory-morphism packaging might help state reusable judgments across foundations, but a view preserving declared symbols is useful here only if its translation and checking account for the used native paths, chosen operations and observations. The preservation inventory and ADR 0002 likewise allow relations and comparison witnesses rather than forcing all clients into total functions and final predicates.

## Defects, risks and the decisive tests

There is no established defect in the inspected native construction. There are established boundaries that a new preservation claim must respect: context substitution is internal to one semantics; permutation observation classifies two-path existence rather than selected bodies; the structured-package correspondence is restricted to its discrete fragment. No proof inspected supports a general semantic morphism preserving the selected universe data.

The smallest decisive negative theorem is proposed as follows. For any proposition `P`, any erasure `erase : Term (loops Bool) → P`, and any observer `decode : P → Bool`, it is impossible for `decode (erase p)` to equal actual transport of `true` for every closed Boolean loop `p`. Proof irrelevance makes the two erased inputs equal; the identity and negation instances force `true = false`. This has inhabited source examples and identifies the failed observation-factorization hypothesis. It does not assume that erasure itself cannot exist, nor that every set-valued observation fails. Existing Bool theorems supply the ingredients; this general separating theorem was not run here.

For the positive route, prove the mixed-endpoint application square and package square through the actual chosen transport, then composition and reversal for arbitrary admitted closed paths, not only the selected `ua` representatives. This tests whether the bridge offers reusable semantics beyond restating a permutation example. Retain the original complete `E`, selected composite, inverse homotopies and comparison square in these test inputs and explicitly record their target images or absence of images.

A discriminating test for broad adoption should demand two settings with independently constructed fillers and a dependent client whose output includes a selected comparison. Require the broad interface to derive the client theorem from primitive obligations and to compose the two routes. Compare author obligations against direct proofs on the same inputs. This can expose either excessive strictness or unearned witness erasure; it does not presume the present concrete interfaces must win. A Cartesian fibrant rederivation is a plausible longer-term second setting, but its chosen witness connection remains research work.

Risks, not established impossibilities: the bounded interface might become a collection of observation-specific fields; a broad weak morphism might require higher coherence beyond the current clients; exact witness preservation may not exist for a chosen target. No counterexample of two distinct native comparison bodies with identical permutation observation was constructed here, so this report does not claim noninjectivity on that particular source domain merely from target proof irrelevance. It claims absence of a recovery theorem and the narrower checked contracts actually present.

Missing evidence is the new bounded route theorem, its exact composite/inverse/package connections, any second cubical model, and matched author-cost data. Full acceptance, EDD statement review of new claims, exhaustive audit and independent Standards/Spec review remain with implementation. No kernel axioms, arbitrary-family inverse, source importer, Wasm route or #489 adoption is required by this recommendation.
