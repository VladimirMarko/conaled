## Why this route

The decisive existing facts are `loopPermutation_apply`, `permutationMap`, and `twoPath_iff_permutation`, together with #530's exact whole-section recovery. They already connect actual native transport to ordinary mathematical outputs, rather than postulating a preservation operation. Extending the route to mixed endpoints and arbitrary closed path composition will test new reusable capability. A host bijection is a target arrow; it is not a host equality proof between carriers.

The three reports agree, but their agreement does not establish the proposed new theorems. In particular, the current `loopPermutation_concat` is restricted to ua representatives. The implementation must prove composition/reversal on the arbitrary closed paths it claims to support. It must connect its carrier bijection to the existing whole-package result and use the original native equivalence records in selected composite/inverse tests. Equality of resulting host bijections does not prove equality of their source contraction records.

The negative theorem has a direct independent calculation. Native identity sends true to true and native negation sends true to false. Any target observation identifying those source loops cannot admit an action reproducing both outputs. Proof-irrelevant host equality loops are a special case. The obstruction is collapsed observations, not the mere fact that the target is set-valued: permutations retain the distinction.

## Interface decision and boundaries

Retain direct route-specific laws. A small observation interface needs a second actual route before its abstraction cost is justified. A broader semantic model morphism remains a promising way to derive constructor-level preservation, but this target does not provide all the operations such a morphism would need. Adding fields named preservation of univalence would only relocate the proof obligation.

Preserve full native equivalence records, selected inverses and homotopies, and public comparison squares on the source side. Their observed arrow equalities are part of this route; reconstruction of their full bodies is not established. Do not assert noninjectivity of the square-body observation without a separating pair. The existing full-body injectivity theorem remains an unchanged stronger native contract.

The package boundary is the documented constant presentation with PLift proof fibers. Recover the complete package and native section there. Do not claim arbitrary dependent constructor preservation, higher proof-field recovery, all-context interpretation, source conversion or an evaluator. The triple comparison contributes its current two-path/boundaries, without an inherited pentagon or chosen recomputation law.
