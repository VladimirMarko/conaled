## Decision criteria and present evidence

Promote a bounded task when it can demonstrate a missing capability or test a
specific recurring proof obligation with an independently specified meaning and
a rejecting case. A consumer need not already fit the incumbent interface.
Conversely, a familiar framework name does not justify a universal port. The
size and uncertainty of the next deliverable determine whether it is a checked
construction or a research task with an implementation decision at acceptance.

The prior studies often required a second consumer before any new experiment.
That is a reasonable gate for broad infrastructure adoption, but an overly
restrictive gate for discovery: alternatives then cannot demonstrate capabilities
absent from the current client set. The new tasks supply concrete probes. They
do not assert that earlier reuse or productivity conditions have already been met.

## Why support and classical realizability are no longer passively deferred

The [primary-source reassessment](deferred-traditions-reassessment.md) distinguishes
substantive restrictions from avoidable scheduling barriers. The support source's
ambiguous derived displays still prevent treating its full presentation as a
formal specification. They do not prevent the atoms-and-implication experiment
in #377. Its selected derivation data are an explicit additional contract, not
something truth-valued support automatically retains.

Classical realizability previously lacked a chosen principle, pole and observation.
#378 now supplies Peirce control and a finite arithmetic search contract. It must
construct the pole and distinguish closure, properness, logical coherence and
selected-output preservation. A vacuous pole or an ordinary handler calculation
cannot pass. A failure of exact selected-output recovery does not by itself
invalidate every use of classical realizability.

We continue to defer full second-order support formalization, set-theoretic
realizability models and a general extraction library. Their prerequisite and
scope costs are reasons for these bounded first tasks, not evidence that the
traditions are irrelevant. No architecture replacement or general productivity
claim follows from opening the issues.

## Finite interaction comparison after the fourth wave

[#390](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/390) supplies the accepted
bounded games/interaction comparison under #196, coordinated with #36/#235/#290.
The [construction report](finite-interaction-strategies.md) compares independent
finite forwarding strategies with the existing retained-handler semantics.
Actual intermediate-channel matching and hiding preserve selected service
histories for adaptive responders; a return-only quotient deliberately excludes
those histories and adaptive observations. The result supports retaining native
handlers and keeping the bounded strategy model available for composition tests,
without adopting a universal game interface.

The games re-entry condition is no longer simply an unexamined deferral. Further
higher-order or overlapping-query work needs its own legal-context and observation
contract under #196/#36/#290. This independent comparison does not interrupt
#371's remaining #387–#389 construction graph. Probability sequencing, modal/higher
combination, analytic hiding, and source/model admission retain the owners and
specific hypotheses recorded in the fourth-wave and interaction reports; success
of the finite strategy probe does not discharge those different obligations.

## Represented probability continuation

[#391](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/391) proves
[law-preserving sampler substitution](represented-sampler-substitution.md) for
admitted randomized continuations with explicit standard Borel noise. The
retained-value and two-stage laws use the actual generated QBS product sigma
algebra. Independent finite Markov kernels and selected-record controls keep
law equality distinct from sample identity. This closes the scoped replacement
obligation, without constructing general probabilistic bind, conditioning or
recursion; their ownership remains #200/#36. Native kernels and seeded sampling
remain available directly. Exact integration acceptance is recorded separately
by the orchestration.
