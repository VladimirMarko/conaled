# Felix architectural parity: source inventory and recommendation

This is the research deliverable for [#1](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/1), whose requirements were inspected at `updatedAt = 2026-10-17T04:58:03Z`. The proposed implementation work is in [the ordered plan](felix-parity-plan.md). P1–P6 below identify that plan's work packages, not completed implementations.

## Evidence and scope

The Felix baseline remains `6f36d28097db7ea5f5df3263db3e8be08f74e490`; the inspected local baseline is `c17daf5a66b9812fdbc0cb37cd4e9c49b1949ce8`. Mathlib is `56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679` and Lean is 4.35.0, as recorded in [local provenance](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/c17daf5a66b9812fdbc0cb37cd4e9c49b1949ce8/docs/SOURCES.md). All links below pin source revisions. Declaration names identify the evidence even when a link covers a module.

This investigation inspected source. It did not rebuild Felix with Agda or recheck the Lean library. “Implemented/proved” below means a definition/proof body exists in the inspected source; it does not report a fresh successful build. Historical prose and `verification.txt` are not fresh validation evidence. Felix's [All.agda](https://github.com/conal/felix/blob/6f36d28097db7ea5f5df3263db3e8be08f74e490/src/Felix/All.agda) defines its principal import surface; the inventory also examines the separate `Comma.Vertical`, `Construct.Monoid`, and `Instances.CAT` modules. Commented experiments and TODOs do not count as proofs.

The finite initial milestone is: reusable raw categorical operations, equivalence and laws; maps and category/cartesian law transport; general comma squares with products and composition; exact and relational recoveries; first-order and full compiler adapters; and two migrated application compositions. Coproduct/distribution/closed interfaces needed by current higher-order programs belong in the milestone, but an unrestricted theorem that every comma category is closed does not. Full upstream porting and unfinished constructions remain [#8](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/8).

The pinned `Raw.Traced` and `MakeLawful` entries above confirm those two claims without treating the branch tip as the baseline. The [historical comparison](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/c17daf5a66b9812fdbc0cb37cd4e9c49b1949ce8/docs/history/compiling-with-squares-possibilities.md#L140) describes two source grammars and a separation of target operations from semantic laws. `Compiler.Target`/`Sound`, `FirstOrderCompiler.Target`/`Sound`, and `Compiler.Equivalent` confirm that narrower claim. ## Mathlib interface recommendation

Use Mathlib's lawful category theory as the interoperability layer, and retain a small raw layer for executable representations. [CategoryStruct and Category](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Category/Basic.lean#L123) already separate operations from equality laws, but `Category` laws use Lean equality. A syntax tree whose two association orders differ as trees cannot receive that instance merely because evaluation agrees. Pair `CategoryStruct`-style operations with hom Setoids, operation congruence, and laws stated under the chosen equivalence. Avoid a duplicate hierarchy of lawful categories, functors and limits.

For raw representations, construct the hom quotient only after proving congruence and laws modulo that Setoid. Identity/composition descend, and quotient equality proves the Mathlib category laws. Keep compilation on raw representatives; the quotient is a semantic interface, not an executable optimizer or an equality theorem for trees. Require `classOf f = classOf g ↔ f ≈ g`, and lift maps only after proving they respect equivalence. If equivalence is defined by an interpretation, law transport derives laws under exactly that relation. For a preselected relation, reflection of semantic equivalence is an extra obligation. Mere preservation does not suffice.

Mathlib's [Quotient](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Quotient.lean#L104), `CategoryTheory.Congruence`, `functor_map_eq_iff` and `lift` are suitable when the input is already a lawful category. That module assumes `[Category C]` before forming its quotient; it cannot bootstrap raw syntax with only semantic associativity. The minimal raw hom quotient adapter is therefore justified new infrastructure. P1 must prove that adapter's public laws, and compare its lawful case with Mathlib's quotient rather than maintaining two unrelated quotient APIs.

On lawful categories reuse [Functor](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Functor/Basic.lean), [Comma/CommaMorphism](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Comma/Basic.lean#L67), its projections and [comma limits](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Comma.lean#L168). Mathlib's limit construction requires preservation by the right functor, while Felix's raw cartesian comma construction supplies strong comparisons for both interpretations. Expose the weaker lawful theorem through Mathlib and retain the raw construction's weaker law assumptions on the two component representations. Neither direction subsumes the other merely by having the same record shape.

For cartesian interfaces, choose products/terminal objects and use [product preservation comparison isomorphisms](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Preserves/Shapes/BinaryProducts.lean#L93). Do not require interpretation of a product to be definitionally the product of interpretations. Explicit comparison isomorphisms absorb object reassociation and different encodings; callers prove preservation of primitive operations once. P2 should expose the maps and their inverse equations under equivalence before quotienting, then show they become lawful product-preserving functors.

Higher-order adapters use the pinned Mathlib API: [CartesianMonoidalCategory plus MonoidalClosed](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Closed/Cartesian.lean#L12). The former separate `CartesianClosed` API is deprecated at this revision. First-order adapters request only terminal and binary-product operations and their needed preservation. Sum/case compilation separately requests coproduct/distribution structure; lambda/application separately requests the closed operations. Do not assume arbitrary comma categories are closed. The existing exact `FunctionRep` and relational function constructions get explicit adapters, beta/eta preservation, and their original theorem statements.

The raw quotient and comparison wrappers must remain small enough that an application author supplies operations, their semantic map, and primitive preservation proofs, without reproving derived category/product algebra. These are recommendations to be validated by P1/P2's checked examples, not claims of a Lean prototype already compiled here.

## Required demonstrations and migration boundary

Use two examples to settle generality and compatibility. First, construct a comma instance whose common category consists of finite matrices over `ℕ`, with identity/composition given by matrix identity/multiplication and cartesian products by finite direct sums. Use distinct tagged copies as the left and right object categories, interpreted by identity and doubling respectively; doubling maps a matrix to its block diagonal duplication. The product comparison is the permutation between `(A ⊕ B) ⊕ (A ⊕ B)` and `(A ⊕ A) ⊕ (B ⊕ B)`, so a strictly product-preserving-only interface will fail this test. Connectors and both square sides are matrices, not arbitrary Lean functions. Check identity, horizontal composition, products and a nonidentity commuting square (for example the canonical inclusion into the first summand of the doubled object). No exponential structure is requested. This is a proposed checked example, not an assertion that its Lean implementation already exists.

Second, identify `Squares.Rep` with objects of the identity/identity comma over `Type`, and `Squares.Square` with its morphisms. Prove conversions both ways and round trips, preservation of identity/composition/products/vertical composition, and compatibility of the existing exponential construction and beta/eta. Preserve [graph and witness round trips](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/c17daf5a66b9812fdbc0cb37cd4e9c49b1949ce8/SquareKit/Relations.lean#L126), [matrix/stack agreement](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/c17daf5a66b9812fdbc0cb37cd4e9c49b1949ce8/Examples/MatrixStack.lean#L29), [incremental output enclosure](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/c17daf5a66b9812fdbc0cb37cd4e9c49b1949ce8/Examples/ArithmeticPipeline.lean#L102), and [STLC relational preservation](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/c17daf5a66b9812fdbc0cb37cd4e9c49b1949ce8/SquareKit/STLCRelations.lean#L114). Enclosure and stability remain relations; equality of point functions must not collapse different analysis results. The matrix implementation's existing [coordinate encoding laws](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/c17daf5a66b9812fdbc0cb37cd4e9c49b1949ce8/Applications/Linear/Matrix.lean#L24) remain available.
