# Geometry realizations, local displacement and kinetic laws

Implementation synthesis for [#411](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/411)–[#415](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/415), coordinated under [#235](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/235). The comparisons below concern constructed Lean models and checked clients. They do not establish numerical superiority or a general author-productivity advantage.

## What the experiments establish

The wave separates three questions that the original reports left on paper: representing the same framed hyperbolic motion, composing local displacements with justified domains, and specifying a free-particle kinetic law. These are different capabilities. A representation proof can preserve the existing point/frame contract exactly; a variable-curvature displacement cannot be judged by whether it reproduces a homogeneous disk pose; a variational recurrence must satisfy its own mechanical equations.

| Experiment | Constructed operation and checked client | Observations and remaining boundary |
|---|---|---|

| #412 Clifford | Actual even Clifford realization, normalized rotor multiplication/reversion, sandwich action and sign quotient; finite-word and full-turn controls | Same point/frame/action contract as Lorentz, with representative equality finer than geometric equality; arbitrary pose acquisition still needs a chosen half-phase |
| #413 local disk arithmetic | Möbius displacement on a strict subdomain, explicit division formulas, parenthesized evaluation and alignment | Endpoint and differential with intermediate-domain and label evidence; no claimed connection-exponential theorem or arbitrary path transport |

## Concrete reuse and changes to the initial assessment

Lorentz composition is calculated independently as real matrix multiplication. Its `encode_comp` proof uses the real/Hermitian coordinate comparison and reuses `Pose.matrix_comp` and `Pose.gyro_gap`; it does not define multiplication by decoding a pose. The public carrier absorbs denominator, future-column and normalization obligations once. Literal-matrix clients prove image membership and then use the same operations. `Examples.LorentzMotion.compositionSquare` synchronizes with the existing pose square, and `finite_motion` reuses `Pose.motion_composeList`.

The carrier restriction matters. `Lorentz.Realized` consists of matrices known to be encodings of native poses. Its two round trips are proved on that carrier. Wrong-sheet and wrong-orientation controls establish that those matrices cannot be admitted, but they do not prove that every matrix passing those tests belongs to the carrier. This is an exact usable realization with an explicit acquisition contract, not full `SO⁺(2,1)` classification.

Clifford adds actual algebra rather than another coordinate label. It uses Mathlib's even-algebra equivalence and a Pauli representation of the smaller `Cl(2,0)` algebra to establish coefficient independence. The coordinate product and reverse are proved to realize Clifford multiplication and reversion. Normalized coordinates construct units and a boost/rotation generation description. `Rotor.sandwich_encode` consumes the independent Lorentz formula; ordinary finite-word clients reuse the native motion/square machinery through that comparison.

The implemented rotor carrier is not identified with all of Mathlib's named `spinGroup`, nor with every possible even multivector. Its checked kernel is exactly ±1 on admitted coordinates. The quotient preserves geometric point/frame observations while a chosen lift retains sign. A full turn producing −1 is therefore a useful positive control, not a disagreement with the Lorentz result. The external orbifold arrow label is separate from both matrix equality and rotor sign.

Local arithmetic reuses `gyroAdd`, its disk safety facts, the native Möbius derivative and existing quotient-addition obstructions. The new shared theorem is `Bracketing.Tree.preservation`: a domain-preserving map and an admitted local operation equation transport the evaluation and validity of the same binary tree. Its interface requires no associativity. The concrete model supplies two admitted unequal bracketings, a distinct intermediate-domain failure, explicit left/right division and selected-alignment derivative laws.

This generalization is deliberately small. Its current validity predicate checks a unary carrier domain at leaves and intermediate results; it is not already a universal evaluator for every binary relation of admissibility or every chosen inverse branch. The radial construction retains its own `DisplacementDomain` obligations. Neither route must adopt the other's packaging merely to demonstrate its mathematics. The optional local action-groupoid wrapper is useful when a client needs retained change labels and an orbit map; ordinary scalar clients remain direct.

Kinetics reuses the existing metric, `move_velocity`, `move_geodesic`, `move_speed` and `move_transport`. The added work differentiates the actual kinetic and midpoint functions, proves the mixed Hessian and full Legendre derivatives, constructs inverse neighborhoods using Mathlib, and relates the exact diameter action to the integral of native kinetic energy. This closes the specific gap identified by the audit; it does not repeat the already available geodesic proof.

The midpoint residual at exact samples `0, 1/2, 4/5` remains nonzero, while the exact-action increments agree. That difference is now checked against actual derivative formulas. Likewise, the quartic example has a unique recurrence and a degenerate diagonal mixed derivative. These controls prevent native motion agreement and uniqueness from being used as substitutes for the requested discrete mechanical law and regularity.

The radial implementation goes beyond the initial proof-preflight proposal. It constructs the bounded weighted flow with a retained Picard fixed-point witness and its ball containment. For initial-data regularity, `RadialVariation` uses the actual polynomial integral equation on continuous path spaces. Its partial derivative has an explicit inverse, allowing Mathlib's implicit-function theorem to construct a smooth local family. `RadialDependence` identifies that family with the bounded ODE solution by uniqueness. The construction proves smooth dependence near zero, rather than assuming the C1 premise needed by the exponential inverse theorem.

`RadialBranches` restricts the inverse branch to the domain where its exponential is the actual bounded flow. `RadialAngular` relates the selected origin geodesic's parallel transport to the radial formula. `RadialNonassociative.associator_remainder` states an O(|t|⁵) error for the actual nested operation with leading term −2t³. `positive_nonassociative_client` combines a positive parameter, all intermediate displacement domains and unequal outputs. This is materially stronger than the homogeneous arithmetic experiment or a formal cubic polynomial by itself. The result depends on the entire proved remainder-to-flow chain, not only the leading coefficient.

The radial metric reuses existing conformal coefficient identities and norm-square differentiation while instantiating the C4-invariant gradient of |z|². It does not silently substitute the earlier x² metric, which fails quarter-turn invariance. Its circle/hold comparison uses the actual radial connection and solved transport equation; it is separate from the local exponential neighborhood and does not claim the circle is a geodesic.

## Input acquisition and observation limits

These models prove laws for admitted inputs. They do not all provide algorithms that turn arbitrary user data into those inputs.

Lorentz acquisition accepts a certified native pose or a proof that an independently given matrix lies in the encoding image. Clifford acquisition accepts normalized coordinates or certified generators; `ofPose` additionally asks for a chosen half-phase. The implementation does not provide a half-phase constructor for every unit phase, a global continuous lift, or rounded normalization. Noncomputable real square roots remain exact mathematics.

Local disk division has explicit formulas and open branch domains. Its consumer still supplies membership and alignment evidence; an exact real formula does not give a decidable floating-point membership test. Kinetic inverse branches exist around diagonal disk pairs for positive time. Their symmetry comparison checks the original and rotated operands lie in the respective branch domains; arbitrary IFT choices are not proclaimed canonically equivariant. The radial construction similarly retains its actual exponential/log and intermediate-operation domains. Existence of its positive nonassociative parameter does not produce a numerical radius or a runnable inverse solver.

Raw total Lean functions may have values outside all these domains. Those values do not acquire geometric, inverse or mechanical meaning merely because evaluation is defined. This is also why construction and observation remain separate: an endpoint does not determine parallel transport, a frame derivative does not specify a trajectory, equal transport does not determine a clock, and numerical tangent action cannot recover an ineffective label.

The wave addresses the audit's valid-input blind spot by exposing these boundaries rather than burying them in a generic backend interface. It does not establish that acquisition is cheap, automatic or usable by the planned executable driver. No matched runtime, conditioning or independent author-productivity study was performed. Reused theorems establish proof reuse; they do not establish broad author-effort superiority.

## Architectural implications and existing owners

Retain the exact Lorentz and Clifford realizations as alternatives under their proved contracts. Retain direct endpoint/differential use and the small admitted-tree preservation theorem where it fits. Retain the separate kinetic phase relation and local regular example. Retain the actual fixed variable-curvature construction as a distinct geometric capability. These conclusions follow from the constructed operations and consumers, not from preference for a named influence or for the earliest implementation.

The variable-curvature and kinetic routes were not required to emulate the incumbent timed-command API. Their results answer different questions: one supplies local displacement for a nonhomogeneous connection; the other specifies a free-particle action and a discrete variational relation. Neither is inferior for lacking arbitrary controlled turns. Conversely, neither supplies the driver's requested trace/error contract without further work. A claim to replace that driver would need to preserve its declared observations or explicitly change the physical problem.

No current result justifies making one representation, a universal loopoid record, selected evolution maps, or an internal language mandatory. The common construction/observation machinery supports the representation clients, but shared mathematics should not be inferred from the fact that every experiment can be described informally as transport. Degenerate mechanics can retain relations. Future Clifford incidence clients can motivate a different architecture. Those alternatives remain permitted without creating speculative implementation tickets now.

#49 continues to own runnable controlled simulation, subdivision/search, rational feasibility, certified trace output and representative-change replay. Exact representation alternatives and analytic inverse branches do not complete its acceptance criteria. #32/#34 retain geometry, broader simulator choices, numerical realization and general presentation questions. Accepted #408 supplies its bounded nonconstant frame/connection descent, not automatic descent for newly generated mechanical paths or arbitrary variable-curvature curves. #103 retains its separate source-interpretation gates. #235 owns concrete shared-interface mismatches exposed by actual consumers; this wave does not establish a need for a new universal preservation interface.

There is no identified material architecture decision requiring user input to accept these bounded developments. Unanswered questions remain about which future executable workload should drive representation selection, what point/frame error domain it requires, whether it observes a chosen spin lift, and whether its dynamics are controlled, free or constrained. These are real specification questions for such a consumer, not reasons to block the current mathematics or silently select a default backend. If a concrete request makes those choices incompatible, ask for that decision with the competing contracts visible.

The completed wave stops at its declared scope. Preserve the exact gaps in existing owners. Generality beyond the current models, full classification, automatic branch acquisition and another numerical backend each need a concrete consumer before they justify further tickets.
