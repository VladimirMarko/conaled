## Source depth and strongest challengers

The [assumptions comparison](external-discovery-280/assumptions-comparison.md)
checks the primary owner theorem behind Little Theories, MMT's total-view
conditions and the Trocq theorem display that was inaccessible in the initial
memo. It also checks the local-universes hypotheses as a reason D1 might still
need to precede extraction. These are inspected definitions, statements and
selected arguments; dependencies are recorded, not silently treated as audited.

[Johnsen–Lüth](https://ebjohnsen.org/publication/04-tphols/04-tphols.pdf)
provides a concrete route from proof dependencies to applicability conditions.
Its relevance is proof-relative sufficiency. We do not infer a globally weakest
theory or automatic abstraction of arbitrary native Lean proofs. The strongest
organization challenger is a compact author-designed theory with
[MMT views](https://www.kwarc.info/people/frabe/Research/mmt.pdf): totality and
foundation requirements remain part of the transport contract. A mechanically
extracted premise list may be worse than that manually designed artifact.

The [extension comparison](external-discovery-280/extension-comparison.md)
checks dependent alternatives to the initial first-order Morita proposal.
It distinguishes all-model from cofibrant-model claims, equality assumptions,
and unresolved coherence conjectures. The first-order
[Morita result](https://arxiv.org/html/1506.04675) does not promise a natural
representative selector or exact transport of proof objects. That limitation
is part of the selected experiment rather than a detail to hide during adoption.

The [computation comparison](external-discovery-280/computation-comparison.md)
inspects probability and concurrent-game source results, then reconciles them
with [#199's assessment](probability-model-comparison.md). Its additional source
control is an intensional strategy model distinguishing equal output
probabilities. Ground adequacy is insufficient to claim a compositional quotient
for every context. The existing #285 research owns the general comparison with
independently chosen mathematical meaning; a probability extension is deferred.

These checks are enough to choose bounded questions. They do not establish a
complete ranking of traditions. Institution pushout/conservativity trails,
quantitative effects beyond the inspected passages, full proof-relevant logic
translations and more general higher coherence remain qualified leads. No
priority here depends on an inaccessible theorem being true in a stronger form
than its inspected statement.

## What would change this ordering

- If the first source proof already exposes a compact reusable interface and
  extraction adds only noise, stop or demote extraction. If its interpretation
  instead fails on repeated substitution/coherence, prioritize the concrete D1
  obstruction through #107/#235.
- If the required extension adds new structure rather than a representation,
  a Morita certificate may be the wrong contract. Retain a weaker explicit
  expansion/interpretation obligation. If exact witness recovery is required
  and no descent exists, report failure rather than choose representatives
  invisibly.
- Promote D3 when the author must change effects while retaining a realizer.
  Promote the #199 model experiment when arbitrary admissible random functions,
  rather than a fixed closure environment, become a required client interface.
- Promote a dependent strictification alternative only when its exact model,
  equality and coherence hypotheses fit the actual client. No general higher
  identity claim follows from a UIP/cofibrancy theorem.
- Revisit D6 or a concurrent semantic model when the required observations are
  wiring or interaction, rather than forcing them into returned-value equality.

Remaining unassessed traditions include synthetic domain theory beyond these
sources, nominal binding, proof-theoretic semantics, classical realizability,
rewriting-based proof assistants, and broader categorical systems theory.
They remain candidates. Before a future adoption decision, inspect the omission
most likely to overturn that particular decision. #235 owns that continuing
process; finishing #280 does not claim the literature search is exhausted.

The stopping condition is met by a source-backed next sequence, credible
challengers, explicit failure tests, preserved uncertainties and owned deferred
work. Author benefit, full model applicability and implementation costs remain
questions for the experiments, not results supplied by this research.
