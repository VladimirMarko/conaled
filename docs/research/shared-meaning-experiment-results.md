# Shared meanings, dependent translations and semantic boundaries

This records the combined outcome of
issues #343–#350, following the
institution, dependent-calculus and semantic-tradition assessments. The individual
reports contain the definitions, source hypotheses, checked examples and limits.

## Architectural conclusions

**Keep the shared interpretation explicit and extend it by capabilities.**
The [institution experiment](progressive-institution-capabilities.md) connects
proof translation and computational realization through the same evidence map.
Composition then preserves their agreement. Its parser client retains the
selected parse or refutation, and its dependent client retains the chosen value.
An ordinary satisfaction comparison forgets distinctions those clients need.
The experiment supplies chosen-signature connections, not a full category of
institutions or a universal replacement for existing squares.

**A semantic boundary must preserve the observation actually promised.**
The [relational and selected-path experiment](relational-and-selected-path-boundaries.md)
distinguishes preservation of a selected related value from preservation of the
whole set of possible meanings. Forward relational preservation suffices for
the former; a checked counterexample rejects automatic descent for the latter.
Selected arrows and higher cells remain data. The handles hide redundant flags;
they do not identify all paths with the same endpoints or all witnesses with
the same action.

**Operational assumptions belong to the requested operation.**
The [conversion experiment](joint-conversion-extensions.md) supplies a normalizer
and checked certificates for its bounded equation package. It does not impose
decidable equality on arbitrary mathematical settings.
The [partial-computation experiment](context-dependent-partial-computation.md)
derives selected least solutions using continuity and strict comparison maps.
Fuel exhaustion remains unknown, distinct from a semantic undefined result.
The [synthetic-domain-theory comparison](effective-topos-selected-solutions.md)
instead uses internal morphisms of the effective topos and complete strict
algebras. That construction is a paper result here, not a Lean Eff provider or
a theorem about arbitrary functions on underlying sets.

**Reuse local compatibility before adding another whole-program contract.**
The [reuse audit](compositional-reuse-audit.md) identifies existing square
pasting, law transport, constructor/observer induction, finite execution fusion
and adjoint comparison results. Several current consumers already use them.
New clients still owe their local interpretation and dependency proofs; moving
those proofs into certificates does not eliminate them. Neither this audit nor
successful formalization establishes a measured agent-productivity advantage.

**Dependent compilation needs both syntax templates and typed instantiation.**
The [theory-map experiment](dependent-theory-maps.md) implements independent
projection beta, projection beta/eta and dependent-eliminator presentations.
Finite closed syntax templates compose, and their interpreted action agrees
with successive translation. Closed rule templates recover the contextual
rules in arbitrary ambient contexts. Local certificates derive the forward translations, and admissible
substitution discharges typed applications of their premises. The selected
client keeps exactly the specified dependent tuple after both substitutions.
Generic certificate composition preserves proof trees by retaining typed
substitution instructions until the final erasure into ordinary derivations.
The erasure uses proved substitution admissibility and agrees with the retained
translator on source proofs. The identity and composition laws concern actual
certificate trees, not merely the existence of a proof. Erasing between arbitrary
stages is a separate optimization requiring coherence; sequential execution
alone does not establish it. Reflection, reverse compilation and full model
soundness also remain separate claims.

The [semantic-traditions inventory](semantic-traditions-coverage.md) records the
remaining leads with their existing owners and concrete promotion conditions.
These include nominal binding, proof-theoretic support, classical realizability,
relational hiding, local universes, probability and quantitative observations.
They remain distinct mathematical questions; the adjoint experiments do not
supersede them. Higher representation comparisons remain under #200/#235, and
broader institution adoption and the deferred general executable proof
translator remain under #290 with #339's consumer condition. #107 remains
paused and #108's independent task remains unselected.

No repository-wide migration follows from these experiments. Their results
support smaller, independently useful interfaces and explicit local proof
obligations. Broader adoption should be tested against a client whose required
observations and dependencies exceed the current checked cases.
