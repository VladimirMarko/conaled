# Foundations retrospective

Retain the constructed libraries and their native specializations. The waves have established reusable, conditional mathematical structure across several settings. They have not established the intended general authoring language, a universal preservation framework, or that independently admitted fragments can be combined in one model. The next assessment should test those joints rather than use another completed bounded ticket as a proxy for them.

## What we learned

**The contract has to identify the retained observation and direction.** Selected paths, full histories, probability laws and dependent values cannot be reconstructed from their truth/support images. But coarser observations can themselves be valid specifications. The finite-strategy quotient intentionally forgets query history; Horn support intentionally differs from labelled derivation trees; the control delivery pole can be adequate while accepting an answer that fails first-match selection. Retain these positive coarse contracts. The lesson is to expose the choice, not to make every model retain maximal data.

**Model construction is a separate theorem obligation from organization of interpretations.** The institution capabilities compose supplied evidence maps. The SDT chain constructs the objects and uniform arrows that make its promised maps exist. These solve different problems. The probability model is another instance where assembly of semantic structure was the work, not a missing adapter. The earlier practice of demanding a second incumbent client before admitting alternatives would have excluded exactly this evidence.

**Universal statements require the right quantifier domain.** The all-context SDT result is stronger than per-point witnesses; arbitrary fragment induction is stronger than examples; current universal claims are still restricted by their grammar and model class. The first-wave control repair exposed a pure-term substitution argument being used on mixed syntax with open runtime continuations. Structural coherence was initially omitted in the linear institution experiment and repaired with pentagon/triangle/hexagon laws and a rejecting twisted algebra. These are useful process lessons: inspect the intermediate syntax and construct independent models, rather than infer completeness from a theorem name or passing finite examples.

## Remaining goal and neglected integration

The largest unresolved foundation joint is combining independently admitted dependent, higher and smooth structure while preserving the author's actual source statement. `Examples/HigherSharedObservation.lean:22–52` decodes the actual twice-substituted target witness and observes 1 rather than reflexivity's 0. That is real higher-data preservation. It does not provide arbitrary higher constructor admission. Conversely, `SquareKit/Internal/DesignatedMap/Extensional.lean:1–5` explicitly says its quantified native theorem is not a source-calculus interpretation, and `GroupoidControl.lean:78` provides a non-recovery control for selected gluing. The corresponding gap is documented in `docs/research/architecture-scaling-study/results.md`: #289/#290 own unchanged-source interpretation and connected-model comparison; #100/#36 own joint higher/smooth admission. None is completed by either a successful transport wrapper or the bounded negative encoding result.

Inference: the programme has accumulated a useful collection of preservation principles and independently admitted fragments faster than it has resolved their joint admission and author-facing composition. This is not evidence that the fragments are incompatible. It is a warning against substituting their separate success for a model where their constructors and comparisons coexist. A common theorem subsuming them is not automatically desirable, but an end-to-end intended client has to account for every seam.

The user goal also asks authors to discover required capabilities and reuse arguments at scale. Completion of finite examples, all-term proofs, and three cumulative trial scales do not establish this. `docs/research/architecture-scaling-study/results.md` reports reuse and localized repair on both native and shared routes, with mixed timing evidence and limited controls. `docs/research/construction-observation-research-assessment.md` separates given-interface use from discovering a signature or compatibility law. The foundation-level missing evidence is whether a new argument can expose its genuine assumptions without exporting the encoding bureaucracy to its author. This remains an unanswered question, not a proved failure.

Operational correspondence remains another unclosed class of joints. A retained event list is not independent evidence that hardware executed an operation; a tracked map is not an adequacy theorem for a chosen runtime; exact rational numerical error is not floating-point soundness. Those distinctions are documented and should remain visible in claims about a verified compiler/system. Owners include #196/#225 and the concrete adapters. The current issue snapshot also shows #193 is closed, despite older reports retaining it as a resource owner, so those historical ownership references should not be presented as active investigation without reconciliation.

The snapshot has #371/#389/#391/#417 closed, and #8/#36/#100/#200/#235/#288/#289/#290 open. ## Discriminating assessments before another schedule

1. Trace one intended new argument through unchanged source, its primitive requirements, a genuinely admitted model, two composed setting changes, and the required selected/coarse observation. Prefer the unresolved joint higher/smooth or dependent-model seam. Classify each arrow as constructed, assumed, derived, or missing; preserve a native baseline. This can reveal exactly where the language ambition still fails without designing a universal framework first.
2. Compare total effort for a fresh extension that changes a law and a dependent observation, including model admission and repair of existing clients. Both native and shared authors should reuse public generic theorems. Freeze the contract independently and record assistance and resources. This tests the stated agent-productivity goal instead of theorem count or handpicked numerical scale.
3. Audit the handful of semantic-to-execution seams required by one deliverable system, naming physical correspondence, runtime adequacy, numerical representation and checker assumptions separately. Choose acceptance evidence per seam; do not count #417's cached equation or the SDT existence of trackers as proof of the other seams.

These are proposed assessments, not new implementation scheduling or issue creation. No mathematical defect was established in the inspected current declarations. The principal finding is a mismatch between the breadth of completed bounded constructions and the still-unproved combination, source-admission and author-reuse claims needed for the long-term goal.
