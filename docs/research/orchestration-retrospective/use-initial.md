# Independent use and scaling assessment

Retain the compositional library and native entry points. The waves produced usable proof infrastructure, independent model constructions, and several bounded executable systems. They have not yet established that a new author can assemble a large mixed setting cheaply, or that the architecture lowers total development cost. That distinction should govern the retrospective, rather than either counting closed tickets as generality or dismissing checked fragments as toys.

This report inspected the pinned source and committed evidence without builds or new experiments. No other current retrospective assessment was read and no conclusions were coordinated. Historical architecture and consumer reports named in the brief were consulted. Historical acceptance receipts are evidence reported by those runs, not reruns here. The supplied issue snapshot was used for status; it marks #371/#389/#417 and #49 closed, with #235/#289/#290/#36/#100 and #34 open.

## What we learned about author effort

The evidence supports reuse and local change containment, including in native code. It does not establish broad productivity superiority of the shared interface.

The #358 trial is the clearest scaling evidence: both authors completed three cumulative scales and repair; exact submissions replayed and were separately audited. I inspected `experiments/dependent-operation-scaling/evidence/setup/358-replay-report.md`, which records all eight passing snapshots, original failures and the repaired coordinator audit harness. The report's distinction between accepted proofs and failed attempts is credible and useful.

Both authors supplied primitive meaning, instructions and independent machine laws. Both reused their generic preservation/substitution proofs through repair. Native reported less total stage time; shared reported fewer checks and slightly lower late-stage times (`docs/research/dependent-operation-scaling.md:168` and `:233`). One author per route, cumulative coupled dimensions, anticipated later tasks, unknown model configuration and excluded preparation effort prevent causal or asymptotic conclusions. Reported times are not exact total labor. Larger constants in the same experiment would not resolve these limitations.

Earlier trials provide different evidence rather than replications of the same scaling result. Toolkit authors could use existing DList helpers and extend to a reverse-list representation, but they still owed decode laws (`docs/research/toolkit-author-effectiveness.md:97`). Requirement-discovery authors could publish reusable requirements and reject missing laws; the manual author found a weaker witness-observation requirement than the supplied sufficient interface (`docs/research/proof-requirement-author-trial.md:137`). This is evidence that prepared tooling can also steer authors toward stronger assumptions. No general claim about humans, cheaper models, source-language ergonomics or automated extraction follows. #107 is paused, #108's independent task is unselected, and the broader surface comparison remains #116/#117.

## What is neglected or still unmeasured

**Model assembly and input acquisition are the largest demonstrated unfinished bridges.** External Agda/Myers feedback requests an exact theorem in Agda, selected mapping comparisons and a simultaneous higher/smooth setting. Reusing a Lean theorem there means reconstruction and checking, not importing it as a premise (`docs/research/architecture-scaling-study/orbifold-consumer-assessment.md:10`). Existing sheaf, manifold, torsor and higher witnesses are useful components; their coexistence as modules does not construct the required joint interpretation (`:93`). Owners #100/#36 and #289/#290 remain appropriate. This is an unmet ambition, not a contradiction of the checked component theorems. The external consumer assessment should not be described as adoption or an independently benchmarked usability result.

The later geometry wave makes acquisition costs explicit. `Applications/Orbifold/Lorentz.lean:234` defines `Realized` by membership in the encoding image. Its multiplication uses actual matrix multiplication (`:268`), and `encode_comp` proves the connection (`:172`); the carrier does not give an algorithm admitting any putative Lorentz matrix. Clifford half-phase acquisition, local displacement domains and noncomputable inverse branches pose analogous caller work (`docs/research/geometry-realization-experiment-results.md:43`). These alternatives establish more mathematics without yet replacing the runnable rational driver. Connecting exact admission to useful numerical acquisition remains a concrete consumer question under #34/#32, rather than grounds to demand a universal backend interface.

**Total orchestration and maintenance cost is not measured.** The trials preserve failures, validation, replay and preparation records, but earlier framework design time is unknown; setup and coordinator costs are outside measured author time (`docs/research/dependent-operation-scaling.md:200`). A project that completes many bounded implementations may still impose growing discovery, adapter, review and migration costs. That is a plausible risk, not an observed scaling failure. The current records cannot answer cost per successfully reused theorem, time to locate a suitable interface, or maintenance cost when several models change together. Broad issue owners preserve visibility; they do not demonstrate active investigation or measured integration progress.

**Combined use is less tested than individual capability.** Syntax, typed interpretation, operational machines, cost models, probability, effects, geometric domains and physical IO have separate contracts. Successful pairs do not automatically form a verified mixed application. The reports often state this accurately: #353 adds optional fusion; #390 adds finite interaction hiding without a higher-order language; #391 handles admitted sampler substitution without general bind/conditioning/recursion; #371 supplies a selected internal solver without a machine adequacy result. The neglected question is which actual application needs these to meet, and what new obligations appear there. A demand that all be fused immediately would itself be speculative.

**Development acceptance and execution trust remain different work.** The repository has invested in exhaustive audits, immutable-candidate bookkeeping and fresh checks. The integrity guard remains a local mitigation with direct/editor bypasses and noncryptographic trace hashes (`README.md:231`); #222 and #43 remain open. #417's exact checker acceptance does not solve those development-cache problems. Its proof-generation normalization failures also show why proof construction cost deserves separate measurement (`docs/research/relative-cached-acceptance.md:23`). The unresolved missing-helper formatter under #418 is diagnostic visibility, not a missing rejection theorem. Likewise, formal arena costs or selected external records do not validate host IO, physical devices or crash recovery.

Produce a premise-by-premise admission matrix distinguishing supplied, constructed and missing components. The decisive outcome is whether a joint model can interpret that theorem, not whether another small example succeeds. No new implementation sequence is implied.
- Under #34/#27/#196, take one already desired executable consumer and audit the entire path from admitted input through computation to observation. Charge input acquisition, rejected attempts, resource growth and trusted IO. A useful outcome may be a precise refusal boundary or evidence that the existing direct route is preferable. This exposes cross-component gaps without demanding universal integration.

No new implementation defect was established by this read-only sampling. The correction is to evaluate the next architecture claim against complete consumer work and retained assumptions. The strongest positive result across these waves is that local mathematical compatibility repeatedly supports actual composition, while the strongest unresolved question is how economically authors can discover and assemble those compatibilities at larger scale.
