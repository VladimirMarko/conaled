# Frozen dependent author interfaces

This report records [#320](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/320),
the comparison of the retained `PathTransport.Typed.Authoring` interface and the
reviewed guarded judgment interface from #315 after the Sigma admission in #317.
Both fresh authors completed the matched task without assistance or interface
changes. Their exact submissions replay and preserve the specified selected J
certificate under repeated dependent substitution and the stable Sigma extension.
The candidate removes model-indexed annotations from the proof author's source
interface, while its missing root-tail facade adds raw-rule work. The baseline's
existing `Motive.reflexivity` already handles branch alignment. These observations
support scoped adoption of the guarded source boundary and revision of its
facade, not a demonstrated productivity advantage or retirement of the baseline.

The [architecture reassessment](dependent-architecture-reassessment.md), its
[mathematical](dependent-architecture-review/mathematical-structure.md),
[implementation](dependent-architecture-review/implementation-boundaries.md) and
[foundational](dependent-architecture-review/alternative-foundations.md)
assessments, [#292's report](dependent-coherence-comparison.md) and its
[external memo](dependent-coherence-discovery/292-external-memo.md) identify
competing diagnoses. The local primary evidence used here is the frozen source:
[Typed author commands](../../SquareKit/Internal/PathTransport/Authoring.lean),
[guarded commands](../../SquareKit/Internal/DependentJudgments/Templates.lean)
and [Sigma admission](../../SquareKit/Internal/DependentJudgments/Sigma/Theory.lean).
The experiment does not assume that a replacement must win or that existing
semantic annotations make a matched argument impossible.

`6ace04de2582151dccfb8005e0f17fd604c1033b` before dispatch; the
[approval](../../experiments/dependent-author-trial/freeze/dispatch-approval.md)
identifies its manifest. [Dispatch](../../experiments/dependent-author-trial/evaluation/dispatch.json),
[initial exposure disclosures](../../experiments/dependent-author-trial/evaluation/author-exposure.txt)
and [completion messages](../../experiments/dependent-author-trial/evaluation/author-completion.txt)
retain the fresh sessions' boundaries.

Both arms quantify over arbitrary full contextual models supplied with the
selected stable Sigma capability. The capability is a stable comprehension
isomorphism with judgmental eta; this does not test beta-only sum models. The
baseline has no Sigma source constructor, so the comparison uses matched reuse
of an old argument at the exact model reduct. The candidate additionally embeds
its source derivation into the actual Sigma theory. New Sigma formation,
dependent elimination, computation and eta are checked separately as constructor
capability, not credited as an annotation-burden improvement.

The calibration is the public one-substitution comparison between an existing
J command and its fresh reconstruction. The held-out argument selects a J term
eta:Id_Q(p,p), where p:Q may vary over the complete based path and dependent tail.
Its branch is reflexivity of the pulled-back p. Source family and selected-term
comparisons cover two arbitrary simultaneous substitutions and their composite.
A retained application uses actual weakenings through k:Id_Q(p,p) followed by
h:Id(k,k); the second family depends on the first generic witness. Interpretation
preserves the value, its type alignment and the selected J term, including after
extension. The private references check validity under the original source
assumptions, rather than relying on raw evaluation equations on invalid terms.

The generic proofs do not assert a truncation level. Their concrete native
specializations provide lower-truncation evidence. No higher-capability claim is
evaluated; #316's source-only evidence does not close the concrete interpretation
gate. #107 remains paused and #108's separate theorem is neither selected nor
prepared by this trial.

## Preparation and evidence controls

The coordinator implemented private references and did not serve as a measured
author. A separate helper tested the evidence wrapper and supplied concrete
reference specializations. [Reference statement review](../../experiments/dependent-author-trial/preparation/statement-review.md)
records mathematical scope and remaining host obligations. The baseline already
provides `Motive.reflexivity` and a reconstructed command interface; that repair
is part of its retained public support. The candidate has an admitted root-tail
typing rule but no named facade method in the frozen `Authoring` module. Its
private reference therefore instantiates that rule. No support repair was made
after the freeze. The reference difficulty is preparation evidence, separate
from anything measured authors encounter.

The public handoffs contain ten and thirty-two local source modules respectively,
as enumerated in their manifests, and exactly those compiled project modules.
Their fresh Git repositories contain no original project history. Each has an
ordinary private copy of dependency packages and builds its allowed project
closure from source. No coordinator project output, private reference module,
original entrypoint or other-arm author source is copied. Frozen guidance,
prompts, tasks, environment files and source allowlists are archived with the
handoffs. These controls are instructions and checked file inventories on a
shared machine, not an operating-system sandbox.

The [checker](../../experiments/dependent-author-trial/check_attempt.py) extends
the earlier #293 evidence method with frozen-input checks and direct import
checks. It archives exact source bytes, commands, separate raw stdout/stderr,
UTC times, exit status and hashes under a fresh attempt name. The fixture report
records successful and failed proofs, duplicate-attempt rejection, forbidden
compiled imports, source/public-input mutations, extra public files and an
interrupted check. It does not certify mathematical coverage, collect a full
agent-session transcript or detect transient edits restored between checks.
Declaration coverage, statement review, exact replay and exhaustive axiom audit
are separate acceptance steps.

There is no wall-clock, token or attempt cap. Authors stop at complete submission,
a documented concrete obstruction after attempted remedies, or an external
resource/interruption condition. Actual configuration and usage metadata are
recorded when available; hidden serving revision, reasoning configuration and
token counts remain unknown. Environment repair and clarifications are logged;
proof hints or source changes become separately attributed assistance. Interface
repairs require a new frozen round and fresh held-out work.

Preparation, reference proof work, support engineering, model/extension building,
measured author work and coordinator repairs are accounted separately. Early
reference iterations lack complete command timestamps and snapshots of every
successful intermediate revision; those records are unavailable. Failed saved
sources, raw diagnostics and final reference hashes remain evidence. Author
attempts use the prospective wrapper. One agent per arm, shared calibration and
a fixed task order cannot establish causal productivity gains or human usability.

## Where the work remains

| Classification | Observed work and boundary |
|---|---|
| Mathematical requirement | Both authors construct the same J motive and reflexive branch, source dependent-family conversion, arbitrary substitution comparisons and the actual two-step dependent extension. Those obligations remain. |
| Model-builder coherence | Full contextual Model supplies substitution, comprehension and J compatibility. Stable Sigma supplies a judgmental-eta comprehension isomorphism and reindexing laws. These are earlier #315/#317 implementation obligations; neither trial author constructs or re-proves them. |
| Baseline source interface | Generic binders retain context, family, telescope, term and substitution codes quantified over models. Interpretation statements spell selected `jCode`, nested `subTmCode` and dependent family codes. `Motive.reflexivity`, source conversion and soundness already discharge the branch's annotation alignment; no new caller semantic equality is assumed. |
| Candidate source interface | Source definitions take derivations under Ω, without semantic code indices or universal annotation equalities. Interpretation is a later step under satisfied Ω. The candidate still writes an explicit source branch conversion and the ordinary semantic preservation statements. |
| Candidate facade | The public rootTail rule lacks a named typing wrapper. The author instantiates it through a local helper, `Derivation.rule` and finite premise cases. This is an observed interface gap, also encountered independently in preparation. |
| Host encoding | Baseline aligns weakening substitution with renaming via `Expr.sub_var_rename`. Both encounter Lean section-variable scope; candidate also encounters namespace resolution and raw-rule elaboration. These failures do not establish mathematical obstructions. |
| Documentation | Existing source comments and examples were sufficient for both completions. A named rootTail method and a short branch-conversion recipe would avoid rediscovering raw rule plumbing; this remains a proposed repair, not a measured improvement. |

The mechanism of extension reuse differs. Baseline's old-language source is
unchanged at the exact underlying full model; its Stable parameter is unused by
that old interpreter. Candidate embeds the derivation and assumptions into the
admitted Sigma theory, proves validity there and recovers exact selected values.
The matched observation is selected-data preservation, not equal source
mechanisms. Original Typed lacks Sigma syntax. No result here credits the
candidate with reduced effort for authoring Sigma terms. The separate constructor
cases use nonconstant dependent families, noncommuting substitutions, selected
dependent elimination and beta, with the frozen eta/stability contract.

Preparation comprised task matching, two reference solutions, an evidence
wrapper and its fixtures, public closure inventories, dependency copies and
inhabited cases. The coordinator and one helper did this before fresh dispatch.
Existing #315/#317 interface and model construction is support effort outside
the author measurements. Measured work is the two isolated archived author
sessions. Post-trial work is exact replay, statement inspection, case/probe
integration, report and audit. There were no author-source or public-interface
repairs in this round. Missing preparation telemetry remains missing; successful
preparation is not counted as a fresh author outcome.

## Decision and next boundary

Adopt the guarded source-judgment boundary for the tested dependent argument
and exact retention contract. It expresses the author proof without the old
model-indexed code parameters and derives its semantic obligations by
interpretation. This is an observed separation of obligations, not a claim that
all author effort is lower. The retained baseline also completes the contract,
and its existing motive/command repair remains a viable interface for its
fragment.

Revise the candidate facade before treating it as a broad author API: expose a
named rootTail typing construction with the admitted rule's existing premises,
and document the reflexive branch conversion. Keep this round's interface and
all failed attempts frozen. A claim that the repair improves author work needs
a new source snapshot, independent handoff review and a new held-out task;
retrospectively replacing the local helper would corrupt this comparison. [#323](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/323) owns the repair and
new trial; this experiment does not implement them.

Defer comparative productivity, human-usability and broad migration claims.
There is one agent per arm, public calibration, a fixed task order, uneven
facade maturity and incomplete session telemetry. No counterbalancing or
replicated population supports causal conclusions. Defer any higher-model
adoption claim until the separate concrete dependent interpretation gate is
met. The generic theorem remains model-parametric, while the concrete checked
interpretation here is native and lower-truncated. This decision neither
resumes #107 nor selects or prepares #108's independent theorem.

retains both independent reviews, exact candidate validation and the integration
record. Final full-spec acceptance is recorded separately.
