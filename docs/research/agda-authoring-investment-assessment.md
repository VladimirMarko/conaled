# Guarded source authoring: evidence and bounded investment assessment

The evaluated #108 attempt establishes that a new independent author completed
one further extant identity theorem through the repaired frozen interface,
including interpretation of the same generated certificate. Continue selective
reuse where a concrete consumer needs that source derivation or its model
correspondence. The evidence does not justify a broad hand-translation campaign,
an automatic third repair/port cycle, or a claim that the earlier dependent-tail
authoring obstacle has disappeared.

This is the assessment deliverable for [#434], under [#103] and [#100]. It
synthesizes the completed authoring evidence. Technical [#433] remains open on
its own construction and acceptance obligations; this assessment neither waits
for a favorable technical result nor declares that work complete. The owner has
requested completion and handoff of started work, with #436 held and no new
wave. The recommendations below do not launch further implementation.

## Four separate costs

**Framework and repair.** #107 supplied general presentation and substitution
mechanisms and known regression proofs before the author freeze. It reused an
already adopted guarded telescope theory; it did not invent a new logical rule
for the selected argument. The work still had to establish dependent context
conversions, squares and selected interpretations. Reports preserve that work
and its correctness evidence, but there is no standardized total repair-effort
ledger from which to calculate amortization. Shorter callers or build duration
cannot supply the missing cost measurement.

**Incremental author work.** #108 used 27 of 40 cycles, including investigation,
local operation helpers, proof translation, debugging, applications and
validation. Its source derivation checked at cycle 10. The author then faced
repeated semantic elaboration timeouts at the default 200,000-heartbeat limit,
explicit-binder problems, a name collision and inverse-label normalization.
All were resolved locally within normal limits. Fresh inversion still needed
its own substitution and congruence laws; the caller still wrote source
substitution, endpoint and conversion expressions. The result establishes
completion through the frozen interface, not invisibility of bookkeeping.
The two full fresh invocations were both consumed: the first interrupted for
an unknown reason, the second passed. Evaluator work is separate.

The category counts and failed commands are retained rather than converted
into person-hours, token costs or a productivity ratio. #106's 40 cycles and
#108's 27 cycles cover different mathematical tasks and different available
libraries. In particular, `inv-inv` uses one arbitrary path and no nonempty
dependent argument tail. The earlier four-entry tail problem was not retested.
Fresh-operation substitution friction recurred in a smaller form, while the
specific annotation obstruction did not have to be solved by this author.

**Model construction.** The technical sibling constructs smooth quotient
deformation families, boundary and chart comparisons, restriction and descent.
Those are additional model costs even when they consume an unchanged source
proof. The immutable [#433 checkpoint at 4944103](https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/blob/4944103646e5ee20ce197069c46d34ef7c0bc1af/docs/research/smooth-source-interpretation.md)
describes reuse of the original first-path transport-composition proof over
arbitrary quotient torsors, including ones without global chart lifts. Its
remaining generated-context/section closure obligations are explicit. A model
at each parameter and descent for the primitive family alone do not establish
a complete parameter-indexed source model.

The coordinating workflow reports that predecessor reviewed and freshly
validated. At preparation of this assessment, integration `b4800d8` was still
under full validation; subsequent source checkpoints `b93a12a` and `d4db39e`
and further bounded increments were not accepted by this assessment. These
intermediate states do not support a broadened completed descent or source
consumer claim. The successor must use #433's eventual reviewed technical
handoff for its final status. #434 can reach an authoring-evidence conclusion
while those technical obligations remain open.

**Demonstrated reuse.** #107 reused known arguments through the repaired
abstractions. #108 independently added a fresh operation and theorem, then
interpreted the same source certificate for arbitrary ordinary groupoid
families and faithful/ineffective C4 choices. The latter retain nonzero arrow
labels even when fiber action is trivial. This is checked source-derivation
reuse with explicit correspondence. The primitive action choices still lie
inside ordinary groupoid semantics; they do not demonstrate a distinct higher
logic. The #433 checkpoint adds a geometric consumer of the earlier source
proof, subject to its remaining model boundary. Broader reuse and its net cost
remain prospective.

## Direct ordinary mathematics and frontend options

The evaluator's direct Lean result uses pinned Mathlib inversion infrastructure.
It already works for arbitrary groupoid arrows and the corresponding
section-object equations. If that ordinary equation is the consumer's whole
requirement, a generated-source translation adds obligations without supplying
a needed new conclusion. The source route is justified by requirements such as
retaining a generated derivation, interpreting its actual selected certificate,
or reusing it through a separately validated model. The direct artifact and the
source artifact therefore have different contracts; their lengths and check
counts are not a productivity comparison.

The [bounded elaborator](bounded-elaboration.md) and
[reference calculus](reference-dependent-calculus.md) improve the range of
public authoring and checking interfaces. Neither automatically elaborates this
Agda identity proof with its contextual substitution obligations. The
[Agda internal-declaration inspection](agda-internal-retention.md) identifies a
possible input boundary before runtime erasure, but it supplies no general
importer or complete certified export. A frontend could reduce hand-written
binding, conversions and recipe expansion only where its elaboration and
preservation contracts establish those connections. Familiar syntax alone
cannot prove dependent substitution, source correspondence, selected-witness
preservation or model soundness.

No new frontend investment is committed by this assessment. Existing #115
inspection, #116 surface design and #117 prospective comparison scopes already
own those questions. Any later experiment should name a consumer, supported
fragment, preserved judgments and the author work it intends to remove, then
receive a separate scope decision. The current evidence does not favor adding
another unbounded translation layer merely to obtain a shorter example.

## Decision and successor boundaries

Recommend retaining the repaired interface and independently checked theorem
as usable evidence for **bounded selective source reuse with an identified
consumer**. Preserve the ordinary native route for consumers whose needs it
already meets. Do not claim that the repair made general library translation
cheap, that all dependent-tail friction is solved, or that nil-tail success
predicts higher/cubical portability.

The following work already has owners; this assessment creates no duplicate
issues or automatic implementation authorization:

- #433 owns the unfinished technical smooth interpretation, generated-context
  comparisons/descent and its acceptance evidence. Finish or hand off that
  started work under its own specification; #434 supplies no waiver.
- #108 delivery owns publication of the sealed evidence and evaluation on
  #102/#100 and the decision record on #103 after independent review.
- #116/#117 retain possible surface-design and authoring-comparison work. A
  nonempty-tail usability experiment or further repair cycle would require a
  separate explicit decision, not continuation of the sealed #108 budget.
- #436 remains held under the owner's current instruction. No new wave follows
  from the favorable result, and pending successor decisions must remain
  visible in the coordinating handoff.

The bounded investment assessment is complete as evidence and recommendation;
independent Standards/Spec review and tracker publication belong to the enclosing
delivery workflow. A positive author result does not close #433 or all of #103.
The older historical policy tying smooth work to a favorable retest remains in
its immutable archive; the approved #433/#434 split governs current work.
