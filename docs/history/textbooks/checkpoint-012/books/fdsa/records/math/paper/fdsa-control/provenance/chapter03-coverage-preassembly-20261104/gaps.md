## Quantifiers, execution models and boundaries to retain in reviews

- **Occurrences and ranks:** count equivalence uses a strict upper count. Most selection/pivot bounds support duplicates; only sensitivity and exact minimum-pivot trace formulas require distinctness. Book index `k` maps to public rank `k+1`; public rank0 remains failure. Invalid measured guards have their own explicit costs.
- **Actual nested algorithm:** the private dependent worker carries membership before correctness is used, avoiding circular termination reasoning. Actual preparation computes every chunk median through insertion-based measured calls, then recursively selects among them; it does not sort all chunk medians as a fast-algorithm substitute. Exact branch equations charge the cached entry length once and only the traversals and recursive child actually reached.
- **Uniform cost constants:** the book selector, fixed-rank selector, extrema reduction, concrete paid median policy, sufficient-group selector and coordinate upper/competitor bridge put their constants before payloads, lists and valid ranks. The generalized prefix bound is uniform even when the payload domain is infinite; it covers both actual and scalar cutoffs before using contraction. The general real recurrence allows signed offsets and signed constant toll, with no hidden nonnegative-value premise.
- **General grouping:** positive group size is explicit. The chosen actual algorithm is correct and terminating for `d≥2` and cutoff≥1. The crude affine recurrence contracts exactly for odd sizes≥5 or even sizes≥6; sizes3/4 having coefficient1 is a limitation of this sufficient argument, not a proof that every alternative grouping strategy must be nonlinear. Size1 has an actual self-call obstruction.
- **Random execution:** fresh conditional uniform choice is over occurrences. Successive active sizes are dependent. Scan volume `W` is an actual trace observation and source calls `T` are a different observed resource, related by `W≤T≤9W+2`. Uniform choice is abstract and does not claim random-bit complexity or physical runtime. The positive-size tail has natural excess parameter and a non-strict event; the empty threshold counterexample is preserved. No unbounded-family worst-case conclusion follows from only finite examples.
- **Paid deterministic policies:** the minimum constructor is actually traversed and charged; exact counts distinguish active entries from recursive edges. The halving policy proves a trace law for actual execution, then instantiates a concrete median constructor that pays both its index-length observation and the measured nested selector. Singleton median-policy work is 19 (pivot construction10), with one entry and zero edges.
- **Query optimality:** the competitor sees only a transcript of charged coordinate answers, with valid Fin indices and all-input selection correctness. Replay is connected to the derived fresh replacement. The actual upper protocol acquires every coordinate and then invokes the actual measured worker on those acquired answers. The declared tariff is query cost plus source calls, with front-end bookkeeping explicitly outside it. Positive query tariff is needed for lower/comparison bounds. This is neither a universal lower bound for free bulk operations nor a lower bound for randomized competitors or physical runtime.
