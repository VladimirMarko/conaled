# Lessons for the later combined conaled integration

This generation preserves the old common source base and each project's exact
progress. It does not merge the three developments or adopt the newer guidance
pin as code. A later integration should select a recorded newer commit, retain
all source histories, identify overlaps, and form one coherent reviewed result.

## What ADWH integration taught

The complete #149 body and all comments, plus #458/#459, are saved under
`reference/` with retrieval metadata. Successful builds and independent reviews
of one selection did not establish exhaustive capability preservation. The
recovered ADWH continuation needed a separate preservation audit and source/book
reconciliation after integration. The handoff retired old private process holds
while retaining useful mathematical research and counterexamples:
https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/149#issuecomment-6768833493

The deciding criteria are useful built mathematics, factual correction and book
coverage, and usefulness to conaled. A checkpoint is evidence, not the mathematical
specification. Account for useful declarations, anonymous examples, contexts and
corrections as retained, generalized with checked specialization, replaced with
proved correspondence, corrected, or explicitly owned remaining work. Reconcile
existing ledgers; do not generate another giant inventory. See:
https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/458

Compare domains, assumptions, quantifier order, computational witnesses, partiality,
ordered occurrences/ties, arbitrary initial states, pricing generality, exact versus
bounded costs, demand and uniformity. Erasing output data does not retain a richer
contract. Check elaborated types at coercion boundaries. Correct an erroneous or
unhelpfully specialized source claim with explicit rationale and a checked bridge;
do not silently weaken it or rewrite every proof merely for stylistic uniformity.

Official code correspondence and repository proof evidence do not certify unseen
printed pages. Preserve exact textbook editions, source identities, corrections and
source-evidence limitations separately from compilation/audit status. #458 explicitly
retained that boundary even after acceptance:
https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/458#issuecomment-6802827095

## Concrete integration requirements

- Inspect current conaled/Mathlib implementations and existing issue owners before
  adding abstractions. Historical prerequisites may already be satisfied; #459
  specifically avoids reimplementing the accepted Timing/Amortized work.
- Preserve meaningful direct Lean clients while extracting shared operations/laws
  only where demonstrated reuse warrants them. A similar theorem name or final
  value is not proof of interchangeability.
- Use real downstream consumers with independent intended semantics, including
  empty/failure/duplicate/order-sensitive cases. Keep counterexamples and useful
  stronger results; examples do not replace universal or asymptotic statements.
- Instrument the actual executor or prove recurrence correspondence. Retain tariffs,
  callback charges, complete producer bills, arbitrary initial state and both
  boundary potentials. Equal output values do not imply equal costs. Abstract
  prices do not establish allocation, sharing, laziness or wall-clock performance.
  https://github.com/VladimirMarko/conaled/issues/149#issuecomment-6806477787
- Keep each bounded result's acceptance distinct from whole-chapter/book completion.
  A successful exact audit does not prove the chosen specification is intended.
  Preserve the original full integration base, findings and corrected source claims;
  run current review/gates after rebasing or conflict resolution.

## Risks to inspect, not established defects

VFA: preserve Coq-source domains, order/permutation/duplicate semantics and concrete
representation invariants when adapting to shared owners.

FDSA: do not silently import HOL nonempty-type/default-value assumptions. Preserve
valid-rank/index bridges, explicit source costs, all unmerged Chapter 4 drafts and
their original review base. Their source-only status remains explicit.

PFDS: distinguish persistent versions, lazy demand/scheduling and sharing from
eager Lean execution. Potential bounds require their specified trajectories,
initial conditions and cost model; extensional sequence equality is insufficient.

Future planning must read current #149/#458/#459 and local owners again; saved issue
comments describe dated scopes, not permanent remaining-work lists. Keep source
recovery complete and durable before starting that extended phase.
