## 1. A possible clean positioned-rotation bound without the path-length term

Original source: `restored/conaled-textbooks-136-progress-2026-11-23/evidence/position-model__independent-check.md`,
the section “Termination and conservative raw time” (lines 97–115). The current
source already owns the all-state natural countdown, exact remaining clock,
represented clocks, physical potential, and actual sharp/caller cases. Its
clean entry bound includes `3*L + P + 5`, where L is source-word length and P is
the supplied path length. The original note contains the additional proposed
accounting bound **`3*L + 5`**, independent of P:

- Before transfer, each activation consumes an original source Tag except for
  at most one empty-path probe and one terminating failed observation. Charge
  at most `L+2` activations. The right scanner inserts its marker only while
  leaving these phases.
- Saved-prefix and scratch stores contain disjoint consumed original Tags.
  Their combined transfer pops cost at most L; their empty probes cost at
  most two more activations.
- Pending starts empty; each pending push can be charged to a consumed original
  node. Final pending cleanup therefore costs at most `L+1`.

The sum is `3L+5`. A proof would need actual provenance/disjointness invariants
through every relevant finite statement and the real first-handoff clock. It
must retain literal rollback and the unconsumed path suffix; it cannot count
all supplied path bits as consumed. This audit did not prove that accounting.
The old model checked finite cases, and its note explicitly did not claim a
universal Lean theorem. The existing required `4L+P+12` bound and exact cases
must not be changed in order to pursue this optional strengthening.

The alternative lexicographic well-foundedness proposal in that note uses
phase ranks 5,4,3,2,1,0 with respective second coordinates
`source.length+path.length`, `source.length`, `scratch.length`,
`pending.length`, `saved.length`, and 0. The implemented natural countdown
already supplies total evaluation. The lexicographic sketch is an alternative
proof idea, not another unfinished obligation or a reason to retain all raw
model traces.
