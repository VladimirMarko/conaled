To: the conaled maintainers and project contributors
Date: 2026-11-09

1. Main request

The most useful next contribution for our constructive Agda orbifold work is
a generic finite-action version of the external presentation already built
for the quarter-turn disk: construct the smooth action groupoid with objects
M and arrows G × M, prove that source and target are local diffeomorphisms,
and prove Mathlib's exact IsProperMap predicate for the anchor. The inputs
should expose finite group data, the manifold structure, and the action by
diffeomorphisms. Neither compactness of M nor effectiveness of the action
should be required.

This is a request to extract and generalize a successful concrete theorem.
The existing disk construction supplies real geometric content, and the
ordinary chart-arrow comparison retains actual arrows and their labels.
Those results give a strong starting point. We did not identify the generic
finite-action proper étale construction in the bounded source inspection;
this is not an assertion that no such result exists anywhere in the project.
If it is already available under another interface, its exact declaration
and hypotheses would answer the main request. [2–5]

The external theorem is useful independently of a full higher/cohesive model.
It gives the Agda work a precise target and separates the finite, smooth,
topological, and categorical obligations that a constructive translation
must discharge. A generic joint semantic framework is not a prerequisite
for this finite-action result.

2. What the inspected implementation already achieves

PrincipalLabelData is a useful explicit-data interface. A locally constant
label is specified independently; the supplied smooth functions describe
its fibers through the local nonzero/fiber equivalence. Coverage, local
compatibility, a glued section, recovery of the specified label, uniqueness,
independence of the supplied descriptions, restriction naturality, and
multiplication compatibility are then derived. The record does not assume
the desired section or its comparison with the existing section. This is
the right separation between inputs and conclusions. [2]

The remaining classical dependencies are also documented precisely. The
gluing construction still selects from unique existence, and point
injectivity still uses analytic refinement machinery. The published axiom
ledger reports propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound for the inspected
declarations. We have read that ledger, not rerun it. Supplying fiber
functions is therefore useful interface work; it is not, by itself, a
constructive port or evidence that the transitive axiom set has decreased.
[2]

DiskPresentation constructs the actual arrow manifold C4 × Disk. Its source
and target are (g,x) ↦ x and (g,x) ↦ g • x. It gives fixed-label source
charts, target charts obtained using the action diffeomorphism, smooth
unit/inverse/composition maps, coordinates on composable pairs, and the
comparison with action-groupoid homs. The exported proper_etale theorem
contains constructed proofs of local diffeomorphism and properness; these
are not caller-supplied fields. [3]

The properness argument is particularly reusable. The finite label space
is compact, so projection G × M → M is proper. The anchor is continuous,
and its composite with the first projection M × M → M is that source
projection. The Hausdorff intermediate space permits the existing
isProperMap_of_comp_of_t2 argument. In the disk instance, no compactness of
the open disk is assumed and no inference from finite stabilizers replaces
this proof. The predicate is Mathlib's IsProperMap, with its Bourbaki
cluster-point lifting contract. [3,6]

SmoothDiskPresentation adds more than a pointwise label correspondence.
Its smoothChartEquiv identifies the actual quotient chart arrows with all
smooth maps from an admitted ordinary principal-open domain into C4 × Disk
having the specified source and target. The reverse direction obtains
local constancy of the label from continuity into the discrete group. Both
inverse laws retain actual arrows. Identity, composition, inverse, and
restriction are compared through the real constructions. The quotient
label and forward action label are inverses, and the generic composition
law correctly sends an arrow labelled g followed by h to h*g. [4]

The controls are valuable: distinct origin isotropy arrows share anchor
values; nonzero disk points have trivial stabilizers; the disconnected
sign-domain arrow has varying labels; ineffective labels remain distinct;
and the noncommuting S3 example detects reversed multiplication that C4
would conceal. These should remain clients of the generic extraction. The
canonical recovery comparison also deserves its stated scope: it treats
canonical data of global chart objects along the admitted split covers.
It does not already provide arbitrary change of cover for independently
supplied descent data or all manifold bases. [4,5]

3. Proposed generic theorem and acceptance criteria

Please expose a construction with the following explicit inputs:

  • A finite group G, with usable finite data such as Fintype G and
    DecidableEq G, together with its discrete topology. The intended finite
    interface should expose enumeration and its completeness, rather than
    leave constructive users only an unspecified finite-existence witness.
  • A Hausdorff smooth manifold M, with its model, charts, smoothness class,
    and any additional manifold conventions stated in the signature.
    Second countability should be explicit if required by the chosen
    notion of Lie groupoid. M need not be compact.
  • A group action on M by supplied diffeomorphisms, with the identity and
    multiplication laws. The action may have a nontrivial kernel.

The desired outputs are concrete objects, maps, and proofs:

  A. Construct G × M with its zero-dimensional label/product manifold
     structure. Define source, target, units, inverses, and composition.
     For a left action, use the order

       (g,x) followed by (h,g • x) = (h*g,x).

     Identify composable pairs with (G × G) × M and prove smoothness of the
     structure maps in those coordinates. Relate these operations to the
     actual action groupoid and prove both directions of the hom/anchor-
     fiber equivalence.

  B. Construct the source partial diffeomorphism on each open component
     {g} × M and the target partial diffeomorphism using the supplied
     diffeomorphism for g. Derive the two local-diffeomorphism theorems.
     A record that asks the caller for these conclusions would not supply
     the requested generalization.

  C. Prove IsProperMap for (g,x) ↦ (x,g • x). Retain the exact Mathlib
     predicate and show the actual continuity and compact-label arguments.
     Keep clear which hypotheses give étaleness and which give properness.
     If a compact-preimage formulation is also exported, supply the
     comparison theorem and all hypotheses needed for that formulation.

  D. Obtain the existing C4/Disk presentation as a specialization of this
     construction, with its existing arrow and smooth-family comparison
     still usable. Keep an ineffective action and the noncommuting label-
     order control. Neither effectiveness nor anchor injectivity should
     enter the generic theorem.

  E. Provide the exact declarations, a pinned build result, and a transitive
     axiom/dependency report for the principal exported theorem and its
     immediate comparison maps. Explicit finite inputs improve the API;
     they do not certify the surrounding analytic Lean development as
     constructive. The report should retain that distinction.

The first deliverable can stop at the generic external presentation and
the existing disk specialization. General manifold-base quotient-arrow
comparison, arbitrary descent, Morita invariance, and a complete theorem
identifying all synthetic orbifolds with classical orbifolds are further
mathematical tasks. They need not delay this extraction.

4. Why the properness definition matters for the Agda translation

The proposed translation must distinguish external IsProperMap from the
literal internal Dubuc–Penon predicate used in the current Agda
formalization. In that formalization, with universe levels fixed, the
definition is

  DPProper f =
    (U : Y → hProp) → DPCompact (Subtype U)
                    → DPCompact (Preimage f U).

The quantification is over ordinary, varying proposition-valued subtypes;
it is not restricted to crisp subtypes. This follows the stated
quantifiers of Myers's Definition 6.2.1. Crispness of a groupoid
presentation does not change those internal quantifiers. [13]

The Agda results in ProperCompactDecisions establish that DP compactness
decides mere inhabitation. For a set X they consequently give

  DPProper (x ↦ (x,x))  ≃  ((x y : X) → Dec (x = y)).

Thus properness of a diagonal in this literal internal sense is equivalent
to decidable equality. Under the displayed Kock field and first-order
Kock–Lawvere hypotheses, the formalized smooth ring has no decidable
equality, so its diagonal cannot satisfy that predicate. These are
conditional internal theorems, not a claim about the classical topology of
the real line. The exact Agda entry points are

  Orbifolds.ProperCompactDecisions.compact-inhabitation-decidable
  Orbifolds.ProperCompactDecisions.Diagonal.proper-diagonal≃discrete
  Orbifolds.ProperCompactDecisions.SmoothReals.smooth-real-diagonal-not-DP-proper.

The diagonal argument is explicit: properness supplies compact fibers; the
fiber over (x,y) is equivalent to the proposition x = y; and compactness of
a proposition supplies its decision. Conversely, decidable equality makes
these fibers empty or contractible, and the proved compact-fiber criterion
supplies properness. No choice of global real points is involved.

This creates a concrete obstruction to a bridge that simultaneously
preserves the relevant ring and diagonal, validates those synthetic
axioms, and identifies ordinary classical properness with literal
DPProper. Comparing compactness only on fixed external manifolds does not
discharge the stronger varying-subtype condition. The equality-subtype
warning in Myers's Warning 6.2.20 is relevant to exactly this distinction.
[13]

The simplest external test is the unit groupoid on the ordinary Hausdorff
real line. Its source and target are identities, and its anchor is the
closed diagonal, which is classically proper. This is already a proper
étale presentation. Restricting to finite actions alone therefore does not
avoid the comparison problem: the trivial-group action includes this test.

Accordingly, the generic conaled result should keep its external
IsProperMap conclusion. It should not acquire a claim that finite-action
properness automatically supplies the internal DPProper hypothesis. A
successful bridge needs either a proved comparison for a precisely
restricted class or an explicitly different internal geometric interface.
The obstruction does not refute the existing conditional internal
orbifold theorem, and it does not prove that every intended formulation
of Myers's foundations has no model.

5. Credit for the local-universe and higher work

The more recent documents provide useful mathematical methods. The
comparison from d7debd4 to cce531c contains seven commits changing 21
documentation files and no Lean source; the paper constructions in that
interval should be credited as such. They do not replace or erase the
substantial previously implemented components. [1]

LocalUniverses.lean has actual Type-valued definitions and proof bodies:
parameter/fiber/name families, reindexing by changing the name,
comprehensionEquiv with both inverse laws, the dependent Sigma parameter
object, Sigma and pair substitution laws including the second dependent
component, and displayCurry with its inverse laws and naturality. These
are useful existing results, not a record merely assuming stability. [7]

The new finite-set report goes further on paper: all finite sets and all
functions, explicit chosen pullbacks and their comparisons, slice
dependent-product hom-set bijections, and a parameter object for arbitrary
dependent Sigma elimination. In the sum parameter

  Σ (a : V_A). (E_A(a) → V_B),

the function is essential because the second family is named separately
at each element of the first fiber. For the eliminator, representing the
motive and branch and proving their behavior under repeated substitution
is the useful next obligation. Formation stability alone would not prove
that term equation. The supplied paper formulas provide a good layout
for doing this work. [8]

These presentation objects are not an object-language universe or a
classifier of every admitted type. The finite-set calculation does not
establish universe closure, resizing, univalence, higher identity, or the
required hypotheses for a sheaf or higher model. Direct indexed families
remain a sensible implementation when they already give stable operations;
local universes are useful where an actual model has weakly stable choices
that require this extra coherence construction. [7,8,12]

TwoFamilyHigherReindex.lean also contains real higher comparison proofs.
Its whiskering calculations retain base and fiber 2-cells and their
boundary transports; reindexHigher constructs the higher functor on total
contexts and proves its projection square. Those methods are relevant to
dependent transport, well beyond equality of object maps. [9]

TwoFamilyFullModel.lean deserves equally precise credit. Its fullModel
calls modelFromContractions with rootContraction and
rootContraction_square, and fullSigma uses sigmaStable. The caller is not
asked to provide a contraction or its square. This is a concrete contextual
J/Sigma assembly from imported providers. We have not re-audited that
entire imported proof graph or freshly compiled it. Its noncomputable
Typed.Model interface is not, by that fact alone, an interpretation of the
Agda project's full Cubical/cohesive source language. [10]

The B²G report supplies a useful further experiment: a dependent family
with fiber BG in which a base 2-cell g acts by the actual fiber arrow g,
and key pullback changes the action by a scalar. The total-context
boundary a = b + d*g and the all-cell pullback calculation detect mistakes
that selected numerical examples would miss. The distinction between
base reindexing, which retains the output fiber, and a map between output
fibers is particularly useful. The Boolean access policy is a stated
factorization condition; it is not yet the semantics of arbitrary crisp
contexts or a full multimodal language. [11,12]

6. What a usable semantic bridge still needs

For the Agda project, a semantic bridge must construct the mathematical
objects and interpret the operations actually used. We need explicit
context and family interpretations, comprehension, reindexing, identity
and composition comparison maps, and constructor equations in their
dependent result fibers. Terms, motives, and branches must survive those
comparisons. Genuine higher cells must be preserved where the source uses
them. A set-valued or ordinary-arrow comparison is useful at its stated
level, but does not settle the higher interpretation.

The remaining source obligations also include the precise meaning of
crisp contexts and their substitution, the modal operations and their
interaction with identity, the admitted higher inductive constructions,
and the internal universes with their size and closure properties. An
internal uniform rule witness cannot be inferred merely from metatheoretic
admissibility of isolated rule instances; their correspondence must be
proved. In particular, a uniform flat/path equivalence must be constructed
in the proposed setting before it can be used as a premise of an Agda
transport argument. The bounded access-policy example does not supply it.

We will use the conaled results as mathematical references and independent
comparison targets. The end-to-end proof and its substantive geometric
content must still be constructed and checked in Agda. No Lean theorem is
being inserted as an axiom, and no unverified Lean-to-Agda proof transfer is
part of the intended trust chain. This makes exact statement boundaries,
explicit data, and actual comparison maps more useful than an assertion
that a complete model exists behind an interface.

7. Optional semantic information, separate from the main request

If the conaled higher development already supplies more of the intended
interface, a concise declaration-level correspondence would be useful:

  • Which source judgments does the existing contextual J/Sigma model
    interpret, and where are preservation of substitution and the actual
    eliminator terms proved?
  • Which existing model, if any, combines that higher interpretation with
    the smooth site, the relevant crisp/modal rules, and internal universe
    closure? Please distinguish implemented comparisons from proposed
    hypotheses or paper constructions.
  • Is there an intended properness comparison that avoids identifying
    classical properness with the unrestricted internal DPProper predicate?
    Its exact quantifiers and treatment of the diagonal would be decisive.

These questions do not ask for another general interface before the
finite-action theorem can proceed. A small concrete successor to the
higher paper, if desired independently, would implement its B²G family and
all-cell key lift using the existing TwoFamily machinery, retaining the
invalid-boundary and nontrivial-cell controls. A source-level transport
claim should then evaluate an actual source expression to the constructed
action, rather than only reproduce the selected arithmetic observation.
