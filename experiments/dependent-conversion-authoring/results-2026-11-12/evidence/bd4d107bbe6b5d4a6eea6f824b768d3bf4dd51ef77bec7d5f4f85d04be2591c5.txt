## Concrete matched continuation policy to freeze

Use the same rule for every slot. Initial sanity review checkpoints are 15/20/20/10/10 minutes for the five phases and cumulative 75 minutes of observed session duration, plus 24,000 reported model tokens where usable. These trigger review, not automatic mathematical failure. At a completed task, move to the next scheduled release promptly. At a checkpoint, retain current source, logs, usage, completed obligations, remaining work and observed interruptions before deciding.

For unresolved mathematical work in a usable environment, grant one additional phase-sized work interval, retaining the earlier partial attempt. At its end, record the phase as unresolved and proceed to the next releasable phase with the same session and helpers. The repair change requires its recorded baseline transition as above. The invalid and maintenance tasks still receive their allocations even when an earlier task failed. Apply this rule without knowing which route is ahead; do not reward a successful route with extra unplanned tasks.

For infrastructure interruption, pause rather than classify the phase as mathematical noncompletion. Resume the same session/exposure with the unused planned interval when feasible; checkpoint wall duration and reported tokens remain recorded, including overshoot. Do not infer unobserved suspension duration. If the session cannot resume, archive it as interrupted; any replacement is a separately labeled attempt, not an erased failure. Repeated unusable-environment interruptions trigger a cohort scheduling review before further work, with the same rule applied to remaining slots. Never continue unattended through a checkpoint without a recorded review.

A correctness defect in the frozen service/fixtures stops the affected cohort and requires normal repair plus a new freeze. A failed author proof is an outcome, not automatically a service defect. Finish on phase completion, exhausted reviewed continuation, unusable environment or documented sanity stop. Manual reviewer decisions must record the rule and evidence, not route-relative progress. This proposed rule is deliberately bounded but is not a renewed hard token ceiling or raw-time productivity test; root should freeze it or another equally explicit matched rule before exposure.
